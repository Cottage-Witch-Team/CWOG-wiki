
# Jar

There are a number of **jar types**{.green} in this modpack. This one holds liquids! Up to three buckets worth.
It can hold everything from **Water**{.aqua} to **Soups**{.aqua} to even **Experience**{.aqua}.

They can also function as **fish tanks**{.green} or **cookie jars**{.green}.

Right click the jar with an empty hand to eat or drink its contents. Don't worry, you won't eat the lava.

You can **place the jar by crouching and right clicking**{.green} it. If you **simply right click**{.green}, you will instead capture small mobs such as **Small Slimes**{.yellow} or **Bees**{.yellow}.
