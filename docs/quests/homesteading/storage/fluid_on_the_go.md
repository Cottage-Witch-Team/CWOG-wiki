
# Fluid On The Go

> Baby got camelback

---


Collecting lots of water or lava but don't want to carry all those buckets? The **Tank Upgrade**{.aqua} allows you to **add and remove buckets of fluid from the tank**{.gold}, storing far more while only sacrificing a few slots of the backpack. **Stack Upgrades**{.aqua} also increase your tank size.

Going on a deep mining trip with your mending tools? Consider equipping the tank alongside an **Experience Pump**{.aqua} to store exp and dish it out as needed. Keep in mind, you will need to **fully empty the previous fluid**{.pink} before using the pump.

To get the most of these upgrades, we suggest **checking out the settings tab**{.gold} for each one. 

Your settings will be saved for each upgrade **even if you remove it.**{.green}
