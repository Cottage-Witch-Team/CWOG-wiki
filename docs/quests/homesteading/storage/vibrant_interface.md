
# Vibrant Interface


# Task:

- Acquire Any of: Interface, Cable Interface
---

The **Vibrant Interface** allows your *Nexus* to *communicate with other mods* like ***Pipez*** and ***Create***. It acts like a *mini inventory* that you can connect your *Nexus* to. Any item that gets piped into a **Vibrant Interface** will be *directly stored* inside your *Nexus* storage ^^if your **Glyphstones** &dhave space^^.

==Right clicking== on the **Vibrant Interface** or the **Nexus Interface** will allow you to ==select== *items or fluids* to *keep stocked* in your **Interface** inventory. The top part of the **Interface** inventory *selects which item or fluid* to pull out of the *Nexus* storage and *keep stocked* in the bottom part. Items in the bottom part can then be *piped out* of the **Interface** using other mods.
