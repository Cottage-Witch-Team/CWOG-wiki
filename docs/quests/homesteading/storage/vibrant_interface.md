
# Vibrant Interface

The **Vibrant Interface**{.aqua} allows your **Nexus**{.green} to **communicate with other mods**{.green} like **Pipez**{.blue} and **Create**{.blue}. It acts like a **mini inventory**{.green} that you can connect your **Nexus**{.green} to. Any item that gets piped into a **Vibrant Interface**{.aqua} will be **directly stored**{.green} inside your **Nexus**{.green} storage **if your **Glyphstones**{.aqua} &dhave space**{.pink}.

**Right clicking**{.gold} on the **Vibrant Interface**{.aqua} or the **Nexus Interface**{.aqua} will allow you to **select**{.gold} **items or fluids**{.green} to **keep stocked**{.green} in your **Interface**{.aqua} inventory. The top part of the **Interface**{.aqua} inventory **selects which item or fluid**{.green} to pull out of the **Nexus**{.green} storage and **keep stocked**{.green} in the bottom part. Items in the bottom part can then be **piped out**{.green} of the **Interface**{.aqua} using other mods.
