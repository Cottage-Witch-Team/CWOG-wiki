
# Fluix Covered Cable

> Wire you doing this to me?

---


Having every block directly connected to another can become a bit bulky. Here are some local options for spreading it out a bit.

The **cable**{.aqua} is extremely simple- use it to **connect various **Nexus**{.glow} &ablocks across a distance**{.green}. We strongly recommend you use **cables**{.aqua} with your system instead of **Pipez**{.glow} as it is **much more performant**{.pink}.

The **Quartz Fiber**{.aqua} works like the **Cable**{.aqua}, however it only transfers power and doesnt connect things to the network.
