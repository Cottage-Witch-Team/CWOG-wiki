
# Fluix Covered Cable

> Wire you doing this to me?

---


Having every block directly connected to another can become a bit bulky. Here are some local options for spreading it out a bit.

The **cable** is extremely simple- use it to *connect various ***Nexus*** &ablocks across a distance*. We strongly recommend you use **cables** with your system instead of ***Pipez*** as it is ^^much more performant^^.

The **Quartz Fiber** works like the **Cable**, however it only transfers power and doesnt connect things to the network.
