
# Wireless Crafting Terminal

> Akin to a bag of holding

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Wireless Crafting Terminal
---


So your storage is *wireless*, but how do you *access the contents when you're away*? You'll need to ==craft== a **Resonant Aperture**, of course.

When you craft the **Resonant Aperture**, it will start *unattuned*. *To attune it to your nexus* you must ==insert it into a== **Guardian Ward**.
{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_guardian_ward_wireless.png width:250 height:250 align:1}
After you've *attuned* your **Resonant Aperture**, you must be within range of a **Aetheric Beacon** for it to work. With no upgrades, this is ^^limited to 16 blocks^^.

You can gain *infinite, cross dimensional access* by creating a **Planar Bridge Card** and a **Linked Entangled Aetheric Fragment** to upgrade your **Resonant Aperture** with *(see the next quests)*.

Over time and with use, the **Resonant Aperture** may *lose resonance*, and if this occurs, you can &6place it in a **Charging Gadget** to fill it back up with *resonance*.
