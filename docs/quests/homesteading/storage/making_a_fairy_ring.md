
# Making a Fairy Ring

> A bit of help from the fae

---


Collecting 8 mushrooms and **placing them in a ring**{.green} will create a fairy ring. Make sure to place them on **blocks that can grow mushrooms**{.green}, such as **mycelium**{.aqua}. You can then **drop items into the fairy ring**{.gold} to **create new items**{.green}!
{image:cottagewitch:textures/fairycircle.png width:250 height:189 align:1}

---



!!! tip "TASKS" 

	- Acquire **Any Mushrooms (8)**
	- **Build the Fairy Circle**
