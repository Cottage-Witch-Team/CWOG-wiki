
# Cable Utilities

> Don't get tangled

---


The **Cable Anchor**, **Inverted Toggle Anchor**, **Toggle Anchor**, and **Level Emitter** are all ==placed onto a cable== and exist in the *same block space*. (Meaning in the same 1 block area). **Colored cables** only connect to *cables of the same color*, or **Fluix cables**. 

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_cable.png width:250 height:230 align:1}

Using a **Cable Anchor** on a cable will *prevent any other cables from connecting* on that side.

The **Toggle Anchor** is similar to the **Cable Anchor**, but ==powering it with redstone== will *allow another cable to connect*. The **Inverted Toggle Anchor** is the opposite, *allowing an adjacent cable to connect by default* &ounless&r ==powered by redstone==.

The **Level Emitter** will *provide a redstone signal based on configurable conditions*. To access these, you can &6right click on the **Emitter** to access its interface. You can *define an item for the **Emitter** to monitor (such as a diamond), and &achoose what set of rules you would like it to use*.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/level_emitter_ui.png width:250 height:230 align:1}
