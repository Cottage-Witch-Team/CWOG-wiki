
# Cable Utilities

> Don't get tangled

---


The **Cable Anchor**{.aqua}, **Inverted Toggle Anchor**{.aqua}, **Toggle Anchor**{.aqua}, and **Level Emitter**{.aqua} are all **placed onto a cable**{.gold} and exist in the **same block space**{.green}. (Meaning in the same 1 block area). **Colored cables**{.aqua} only connect to **cables of the same color**{.green}, or **Fluix cables**{.aqua}. 

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_cable.png width:250 height:230 align:1}

Using a **Cable Anchor**{.aqua} on a cable will **prevent any other cables from connecting**{.green} on that side.

The **Toggle Anchor**{.aqua} is similar to the **Cable Anchor**{.aqua}, but **powering it with redstone**{.gold} will **allow another cable to connect**{.green}. The **Inverted Toggle Anchor**{.aqua} is the opposite, **allowing an adjacent cable to connect by default**{.green} &ounless&r **powered by redstone**{.gold}.

The **Level Emitter**{.aqua} will **provide a redstone signal based on configurable conditions**{.green}. To access these, you can &6right click on the **Emitter**{.aqua} to access its interface. You can **define an item for the **Emitter**{.aqua} to monitor (such as a diamond), and &achoose what set of rules you would like it to use**{.green}.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/level_emitter_ui.png width:250 height:230 align:1}
