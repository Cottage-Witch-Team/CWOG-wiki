
# Automation


!!! tip "Task:"
	## Acquire :
	- Import Bus
	- Export Bus
	- Io Port

---


Depending on how robust your storage network is, you may find it useful to *automagically transfer items* from - for example - your ***Sophisticated Storage*** setup to your ***Applied Energistics*** Nexus.

To do this, you'll want **Import and Export Fulcrums**.

The **Import Fulcrum** and **Export Fulcrum** are a must for a busy witch. Consider them to be *upgradeable, filterable hoppers* that can feed *directly into and out of your system*. This is particularly helpful for unloading or loading **Shulkers** and **Backpacks**.

The **Vibrant Communicator** is similar, but instead of interacting with external storage, it allows you to *transfer items into and out of* **Glyphstones**. ==Switch the Transfer Direction to *Network &6or &aStorage Glyph* and &6place your **Glyphstone**&6 in the left slot==. When the transfer is complete your **Glyphstone** will be automatically *moved to the right slot*.
