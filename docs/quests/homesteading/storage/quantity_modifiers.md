
# Quantity Modifiers


!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Quantity Modifier Tier 1
---


The different levels of **Quantity Modifiers** will allow **Sculk Emitters** to transfer and store up to *64 items* at a time!
**Receivers** and **Transmitters** cannot receive these upgrades, but will work with the items sent to them by the **Emitter**.
