
# Storage Bus

> Bigger is better

---


You can easily connect external storage to your system using... the **Nexus Storage Anchor**{.aqua}! 

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_storage_anchor.png width:130 height:70 align:1}

Simply &6place a **Cable**{.aqua} &6and **Storage Anchor &6against a storage block to connect it**{.aqua}. This can then be linked back to the rest of the **Nexus**{.green}.

You can **right click on this block**{.gold} to access its GUI, where you can choose to **limit how items flow into and out of your external storage**{.green} via the functions on the lefthand side. Each one of the options has a helpful hover tooltip to better explain what it does.


Note: once you start connecting multiple storages, you will want to start Prioritising them. **Setting the Priority**{.green} of your storage blocks tells your **Nexus**{.green} **where to store items**{.green} that you add into the system, as well as **where to extract them from first**{.green} when removing something from the system. **Click to the next page**{.gold} to learn about **Priority**{.green}.




---



**Priority**{.green} can be adjusted for each **Glyph Repository**{.aqua}, **Storage Anchor**{.aqua}, and **Vibrant Interface**{.aqua}. You'll find **Priority**{.green} especially helpful for **keeping your storage tidy.**{.green}

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_priority.png width:200 height:125 align:1}


The number here can be **adjusted using the buttons**{.gold}, or by **typing a value**{.gold} into the box. **Priority**{.green} can be a positive or negative number.

**Here's an example**{.pink}: you keep much of your storage in your **Glyphic Repository**{.aqua}. But you have a gold farm which feeds into a simple **Functional Storage**{.glow} **drawer**{.aqua} setup. If you set a higher priority to the **Storage Anchor**{.aqua} connected to these drawers, any time you **add or remove gold from your console**{.green}, the system will **check the priority list**{.green} and choose this location first.
