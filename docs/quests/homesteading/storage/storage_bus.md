
# Storage Bus

> Bigger Is Better

---
You can easily connect external storage to your system using... the &bNexus Storage Anchor&r! 

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_storage_anchor.png width:130 height:70 align:1}

Simply &6place a &bCable&r &6and &bStorage Anchor &6against a storage block to connect it&r. This can then be linked back to the rest of the &aNexus&r.

You can &6right click on this block&r to access its GUI, where you can choose to &alimit how items flow into and out of your external storage&r via the functions on the lefthand side. Each one of the options has a helpful hover tooltip to better explain what it does.


Note: once you start connecting multiple storages, you will want to start Prioritising them. &aSetting the Priority&r of your storage blocks tells your &aNexus&r &awhere to store items&r that you add into the system, as well as &awhere to extract them from first&r when removing something from the system. &6Click to the next page&r to learn about &aPriority&r.


{@pagebreak}

&aPriority&r can be adjusted for each &bGlyph Repository&r, &bStorage Anchor&r, and &bVibrant Interface&r. You'll find &aPriority&r especially helpful for &akeeping your storage tidy.&r

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_priority.png width:200 height:125 align:1}


The number here can be &6adjusted using the buttons&r, or by &6typing a value&r into the box. &aPriority&r can be a positive or negative number.

&dHere's an example&r: you keep much of your storage in your &bGlyphic Repository&r. But you have a gold farm which feeds into a simple &lFunctional Storage&r &bdrawer&r setup. If you set a higher priority to the &bStorage Anchor&r connected to these drawers, any time you &aadd or remove gold from your console&r, the system will &acheck the priority list&r and choose this location first.
