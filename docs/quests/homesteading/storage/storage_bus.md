
# Storage Bus

> Bigger is better

---


You can easily connect external storage to your system using... the **Nexus Storage Anchor**! 

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_storage_anchor.png width:130 height:70 align:1}

Simply &6place a **Cable** &6and **Storage Anchor &6against a storage block to connect it**. This can then be linked back to the rest of the *Nexus*.

You can ==right click on this block== to access its GUI, where you can choose to *limit how items flow into and out of your external storage* via the functions on the lefthand side. Each one of the options has a helpful hover tooltip to better explain what it does.


Note: once you start connecting multiple storages, you will want to start Prioritising them. *Setting the Priority* of your storage blocks tells your *Nexus* *where to store items* that you add into the system, as well as *where to extract them from first* when removing something from the system. ==Click to the next page== to learn about *Priority*.




---



*Priority* can be adjusted for each **Glyph Repository**, **Storage Anchor**, and **Vibrant Interface**. You'll find *Priority* especially helpful for *keeping your storage tidy.*

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_priority.png width:200 height:125 align:1}


The number here can be ==adjusted using the buttons==, or by ==typing a value== into the box. *Priority* can be a positive or negative number.

^^Here's an example^^: you keep much of your storage in your **Glyphic Repository**. But you have a gold farm which feeds into a simple ***Functional Storage*** **drawer** setup. If you set a higher priority to the **Storage Anchor** connected to these drawers, any time you *add or remove gold from your console*, the system will *check the priority list* and choose this location first.
