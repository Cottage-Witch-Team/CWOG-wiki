
# Upgrade your Luggage

The **Luggage**{.yellow}, when **struck by lightning**{.green}, will have its inventory increased.

The easiest way for this to happen is to use a **Ritual of Zapping**{.green}, for which you'll need a **Ritual Tablet**{.aqua} and a **Brazier**{.aqua}. **Place the tablet and right click**{.gold} to begin the ritual. If you want to zap **multiple creatures**{.green}, you must **provide Source**{.green} to the ritual.
