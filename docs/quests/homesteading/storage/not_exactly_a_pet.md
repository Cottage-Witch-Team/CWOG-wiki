# Task:

- Acquire Luggage
---

There are many ways to carry items with you while you adventure, but not all of them are quite as terrifying as a **Luggage**. It has little concern for others, and loves to *gobble up dropped items*. And yet, it's strangely endearing.

You can ==access its inventory with right click==, and ==pick it up with shift right click==. It *retains its inventory* when picked up, and it cannot die.

The **Luggage** can *wear a collar*, which will grant it the enchanted abilities. However, ^^placing the Luggage in your inventory will delete the collar^^, so be sure to remove it before picking it back up.
