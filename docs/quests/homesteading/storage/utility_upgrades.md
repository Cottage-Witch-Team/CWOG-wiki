
# Utility Upgrades

> Live out of your backpack

---


These upgrades will help you take your workshop with you. Each one does what it says on the tin... **crafting tables**{.aqua}, **anvils**{.aqua}, **smelters**{.aqua}... who needs a base? You can live the life of a true adventurer.

To get the most of these upgrades, we suggest **checking out the settings tab**{.gold} for each one. 

Your settings will be saved for each upgrade **even if you remove it.**{.green}
