
# Certus Quartz Crystal



---
To begin, familiarize yourself with the basic crafting ingredients of &lApplied Energistics&r: &bCertus Quartz&r can be found growing in &cGeodes underground&r.

&bCertus quartz buds&r will sprout from &bbudding certus blocks&r, similar to &bAmethyst&r. There are 4 tiers of budding certus blocks: &aFlawless, Flawed, Chipped, and Damaged&r.

With the lower 3 tiers, every time a bud grows by another stage, the &abudding block has a chance to &ddegrade by one tier&r, eventually turning into a plain &bCertus Quartz Block&r.

&bFlawless Budding Certus&r does not degrade, and can be obtained by making it into a &lCreate&r railcart &bContraption&r or with the use of the &lArs&r &bGravity Spell&r. The lower tiers can be obtained by &6mining with &bSilk Touch&r, or by &6throwing the &bCertus Block&r and a &bConsecrated Certus Crystal&r into some &bwater&r and letting them combine.

You can grow buds faster by &6placing the budding blocks adjacent to a &bCrystal Growth Accelerator&r, which needs to be powered from the top or bottom.
