
# Certus Quartz Crystal

To begin, familiarize yourself with the basic crafting ingredients of **Applied Energistics**{.blue}: **Certus Quartz**{.aqua} can be found growing in **Geodes underground**{.red}.

**Certus quartz buds**{.aqua} will sprout from **budding certus blocks**{.aqua}, similar to **Amethyst**{.aqua}. There are 4 tiers of budding certus blocks: **Flawless, Flawed, Chipped, and Damaged**{.green}.

With the lower 3 tiers, every time a bud grows by another stage, the &abudding block has a chance to **degrade by one tier**{.pink}, eventually turning into a plain **Certus Quartz Block**{.aqua}.

**Flawless Budding Certus**{.aqua} does not degrade, and can be obtained by making it into a **Create**{.blue} railcart **Contraption**{.aqua} or with the use of the **Ars**{.blue} **Gravity Spell**{.aqua}. The lower tiers can be obtained by &6mining with **Silk Touch**{.aqua}, or by &6throwing the **Certus Block**{.aqua} and a **Consecrated Certus Crystal**{.aqua} into some **water**{.aqua} and letting them combine.

You can grow buds faster by &6placing the budding blocks adjacent to a **Crystal Growth Accelerator**{.aqua}, which needs to be powered from the top or bottom.
