
# Aetheric Bridges

**Connecting across the dimensions**{.green} requires a bigger antenna than just one **Aetheric Beacon**{.aqua}. To accomplish this you'll need to build... a square! **Build this 3x3 structure**{.gold}, called an **Aetheric Bridge**{.aqua}, consisting of 8 **Aetheric Rings**{.aqua} surrounding 1 **Aetheric Chamber**{.aqua} and **connect it to your **Nexus**{.aqua} &6using a cable**{.gold}. It can be **standing upright**{.green}, like in the image below, or **flat on the ground**{.green}!

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_wireless.png width:1000 height:600 align:1 fit:true}

**You'll need to insert 1 of the**{.gold} **Entangled Aetheric Fragments**{.aqua} **you made in to the**{.gold} **Aetheric Chamber**{.aqua}. **Don't lose the other one - you'll need to place it in your **Resonant Aperture**{.aqua} &ain just a minute.**{.green}

**Make sure to chunk load this location so you can access your system while you're away. **{.pink}

Alternatively, to **connect two systems together at a large distance**{.green}, **build two**{.gold} **Aetheric Bridges**{.aqua}, one at each system. Then place linked **Entangled Aetheric Fragments**{.aqua} into both **Aetheric Bridges**{.aqua}.
