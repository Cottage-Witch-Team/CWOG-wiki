The &bWard Bypass&r is an item which allows you configure the behavior of your &aNexus&r. To alter the &bBypass&r, place it in the &bGuardian Ward&r and &6select the appropriate permissions&r, as in the image below.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_guardian_ward.png width:250 height:250 align:1}

This &bBypass&r is currently unclaimed, and if placed inside the &bGuardian Ward&r, it will apply the given permissions to anyone accessing the system. However, you can further configure access to your Nexus by having your coven &aclaim a Bypass&r with the permissions you've set on it.

To claim a &bWard Bypass&r, a player should &6hold it in their hand and shift-click&r. Once the &bBypass&r belongs to that player, you can &6place it inside the &bGuardian Ward&r Bypass Editor to give that player the permissions enscrolled in that Bypass.

&dA quick note on Nexus owners&r - if you were the person to &aplace down the &bGuardian Ward&r, you cannot deposit a claimed &bBypass&r. This is designed to prevent witches from accidentally banning themselves from their own Nexus.
