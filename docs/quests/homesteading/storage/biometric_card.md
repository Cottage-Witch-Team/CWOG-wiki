
# Biometric Card

The **Ward Bypass** is an item which allows you configure the behavior of your *Nexus*. To alter the **Bypass**, place it in the **Guardian Ward** and ==select the appropriate permissions==, as in the image below.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ae2_guardian_ward.png width:250 height:250 align:1}

This **Bypass** is currently unclaimed, and if placed inside the **Guardian Ward**, it will apply the given permissions to anyone accessing the system. However, you can further configure access to your Nexus by having your coven *claim a Bypass* with the permissions you've set on it.

To claim a **Ward Bypass**, a player should ==hold it in their hand and shift-click==. Once the **Bypass** belongs to that player, you can &6place it inside the **Guardian Ward** Bypass Editor to give that player the permissions enscrolled in that Bypass.

^^A quick note on Nexus owners^^ - if you were the person to &aplace down the **Guardian Ward**, you cannot deposit a claimed **Bypass**. This is designed to prevent witches from accidentally banning themselves from their own Nexus.
