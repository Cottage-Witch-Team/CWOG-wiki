
# Upgrading Your Nexus

Every witch loves to tinker - we just can't leave well enough alone! And to support this habit, there are certain **Upgrades** to your **Storage Nexus** which can improve your overall experience.

They are deceptively simple to make, so long as you have some **Consecrated Quartz** (and perhaps other gems). Form these into a **Tablet**, and then ==carve that tablet with certain components== to create enhancements, which you can learn more about in the following quests!
