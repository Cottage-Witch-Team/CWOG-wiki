
# Creative Energy Cell

> Infinite power...itty bitty living space

---


The **Lodestone Core** is the source from which your entire storage system *will draw its power*. ==Craft one== using the **Starbuncle Wheel** you were given. Don't worry - they'll be fine in there! A **Block of Gold** is more than enough to keep them happy!

Every other block in your storage system will ==have to be connected to this block==, or ==connected to another storage block== that is connected to this block (we'll get into *remote access* later!).
