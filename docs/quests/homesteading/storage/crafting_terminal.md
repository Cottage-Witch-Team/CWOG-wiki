
# Crafting Terminal

Now you've got your power, and your storage. Now you just need a way to access it! Build yourself a **Scrying Nexus** to be able to add and remove items from your **Repository**. ==Make sure to place it upon a cable connected to the== **Repository**. Remember- *everything must have direct contact* with a cable or another part of your system.

If you'd like to be able to craft within your **Scrying Nexus**, you can ==craft it together== with a **Crystal Ball** and **Crafting Table**.

That's it- that's literally *all you need to know* for a basic setup. Everything else is a bonus! If you'd like to *learn more* or *connect up to your existing storage*, check out the optional quests!
