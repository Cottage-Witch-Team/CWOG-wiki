
# More Storage, More Upgrade

> Beyond basic

---


There are two ways you can upgrade your storage, **level by level**{.green}, or **basic on upwards**{.green}.

Assuming you used the reward from the last quest, you'll now have an **Iron Barrel**{.aqua}. You can upgrade this to **Gold tier**{.green} with an **Iron to Gold Upgrade**{.aqua}. This is taking it up **level by level**{.green}.

To upgrade from **Basic upwards**{.green}, you'll need the **Basic to Gold**{.aqua} (or other level) Upgrade. This will, as you could guess, take a **Basic Storage**{.aqua} and bring it directly to **Gold tier**{.green}.

With each tier of upgrades, **your storage improves**{.green}. At Gold tier it has **more slots and an additional upgrade slot**{.green} on the left.
