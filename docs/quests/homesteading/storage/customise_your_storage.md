
# Customise Your Storage

Within your storage settings, you have the ability to change the way your storage behaves.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/sophsettings.png width:100 height:70 align:1 fit:true}

Select the **Barrel icon**{.green} to choose whether your settings apply to **Player**{.green} - ie, &oall&r your storages  - or **Storage**{.green} - ie, this container only.

Select the **A/Z icon**{.green} you can select (with **left click**{.gold}) or deselect (with **right click**{.gold}) slots to be auto-sorted.

Select the **Brain icon**{.green} to **choose slots to be locked to the item currently in them**{.gold}.

Select the **Sword icon**{.green} to choose a slot whose **item will be displayed**{.green} on the front of your storage.
