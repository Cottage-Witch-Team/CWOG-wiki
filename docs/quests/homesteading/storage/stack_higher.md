
# Stack Higher

> Higher and higher

---


Your Sophisticated **Barrels** and **Chests are capable of &aholding more than a stack per slot**, if you use the **Stack Upgrades** on them. The higher the tier, the more stacks each slot can hold.

You can also *use more than one Stack Upgrade*, and they will add *multiplicatively*!

So if you use a **Tier 1** and a **Tier 2**, you will hold 2 stacks by 4 stacks, meaning 8 stacks, or ^^512 items per slot^^.
