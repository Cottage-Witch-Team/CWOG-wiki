
# Upgrading Your Storage

> Let's get sophisticated

---


Basic chests and barrels are fine for a while, but the canny witch knows that **the rules of inventory space**{.green} are meant to be broken. With a little magic, you can easily make your storage bigger - **and smarter**{.green}!

Let's begin with a simple **Barrel**{.aqua}. Place it in the world as usual, and then &6right click it with the **Basic Tier Upgrade**{.aqua}.

Now, once you enter the inventory, you'll see some new additions.

The slot to the left **accepts upgrades**{.green} which allow your storage to perform certain functions.

The **cog wheel**{.green} on the right gives you access to your **storage settings**{.green}.
