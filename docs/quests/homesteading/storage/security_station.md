
# Security Station

> Magical security

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Security Station
---


Although covens are common amongst modern witches, there is something to be said for *privacy*. In order to claim your Nexus - and link your **Resonant Apertures** - you will need a **Guardian Ward**.

The **Guardian** is a spirit which inhabits the mechanical chassis known as the **Ward**, and they will protect your Nexus so long as they are *properly connected* (it merely has to be touching the other blocks).
