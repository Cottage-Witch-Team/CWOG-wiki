
# Extending the Range

By default, **Emitters** and **Receivers** have to be *within 8 blocks of one another* to function. However, you can *extend that distance* by ==placing down Sculk Transmitters==.
The **Transmitters** can also be configured to *accept or deny* certain items placed on their sides. You can ==read more about this== in the Guidebook.
