
# Extending the Range

By default, **Emitters**{.aqua} and **Receivers**{.aqua} have to be **within 8 blocks of one another**{.green} to function. However, you can **extend that distance**{.green} by **placing down Sculk Transmitters**{.gold}.
The **Transmitters**{.aqua} can also be configured to **accept or deny**{.green} certain items placed on their sides. You can **read more about this**{.gold} in the Guidebook.
