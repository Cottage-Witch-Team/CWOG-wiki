
# Obtain a Sign Post

> Show the way

---


Up to two **Sign Posts** can be placed on any fence block, in order to form directional signs!

==Shift and right click== the placed Sign in order to reverse the direction.

If you ==right click them with a compass==, they will point in the direction the compass points to. Useful for spawn points and Lodestones!

---



!!! tip

	- Acquire **Any Sign Posts**
