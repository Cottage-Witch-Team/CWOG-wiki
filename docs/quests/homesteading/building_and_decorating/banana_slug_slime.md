
# Banana Slug Slime

> #powerscantparry

---


Need to clear out a lot of water at once? Why not try a **block of Slug Slime**{.aqua}? It **absorbs nearby water, converting it to mucus**{.green}. If the slime is then removed, the mucus decays as well, leaving nothing behind.

---



!!! tip "TASK" 
	### Acquire :
	- **Banana Slug Slime**
	- **Banana Slug Slime Block**
