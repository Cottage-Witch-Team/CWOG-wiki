
# A Hat In A Bag?

> It came from the sea

---


Hats! Who doesn't live the simple pleasure of a Nice Hat? **Simple Hats**{.glow} is just that: hats, nothing else. These are exclusively equipped in your Head curio slot, and can **only be found through fishing**{.green} (or a lucky neptunian chest).

Each regular hatbag contains one hat of corresponding rarity: Common, **Uncommon**{.yellow}, **Rare**{.aqua}, and **Epic**{.pink}! 

You also have a chance to find **seasonal exclusives**{.aqua}: Spring, Summer, Fall and Winter. These are only available for a 2-week period each (northern hemisphere) season! During those periods you will have a 100% chance of getting a **seasonal hat**{.aqua} with your regular hat!


{image:cottagewitch:textures/variant_cycling.png width:160 height:65 align:1}

Some hats may allow further customizations, such as **dyeing**{.green} or **variant-cycling**{.green}. Both of these tasks can be accomplished **in a crafting grid**{.gold}!
