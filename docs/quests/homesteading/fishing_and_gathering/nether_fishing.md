
# Nether Fishing

The lava pools at the base of the **Nether**{.red} are surprisingly full of life! Using a **special Fishing Rod**{.aqua}, you can **reel them in**{.green} and enjoy the fruits of your labor!
Many of the fish can either be **cooked**{.green} or **processed into other byproducts**{.green}. Use **JEI**{.blue} to see what can be done with each fish!
