
# Bring Them To Life

> Wake them up inside

---


Where are all the fish...? ***Aquaculture*** fish *do not spawn in the world*, you can only ==fish them up==. 

Don't worry though, they aren't dead- only sleeping! ==Combine that fish with a water bucket== to wake them right up.

If you'd like something with a bit more pizazz, you can ==strike a dead fish with lightning== to get its heart pumping. Make sure you have a bucket ready though- or you ^^may end up with a cooked fish^^!
