
# Too Many Hats!

> I'm down to my ankles in hats

---


Maybe you're feeling overencumbered with hats... or perhaps you just have a hat you &oreally hate&r. Time to clean up your storage space and **upcycle those lids**{.green}!

**Place the**{.gold} **hat**{.aqua} **and**{.gold} **a pair of shears**{.aqua} **into a crafting grid**{.gold}. This will give you a **Hat Scrap**{.aqua}. You can do two things with that hat scrap: 

**Combine 4 of them**{.gold} in a crafting menu to get a **new hatbag**{.aqua} of that type:
{image:cottagewitch:textures/hatscrap_bag.png width:160 height:65 align:1}

Or **combine 2 of them**{.gold} to get 1 &oupgraded&r **hat scrap**{.aqua}.
{image:cottagewitch:textures/hatscrap_upgrade.png width:160 height:65 align:1}

Unfortunately, **Epic Hats**{.pink} are not able to be broken down as they are too epic.
