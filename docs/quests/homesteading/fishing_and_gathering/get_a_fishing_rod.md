
# Get a Fishing Rod

> It all starts here

---


While you can still fish with a plain old **Fishing Rod**, you can get much better results by crafting an advanced version, such as an **Iron**, **Gold**, or **Diamond Fishing Rod**. 

At first glance, they don't seem all that different, but ^^obtain a Tackle Box^^ and you'll get more perspective on how much better these **Fishing Rods** can be.

Why use a rod when you can just dive in to hit 'em with your sword? Well, some fish *do not spawn in the world and can only be caught on a line*.

---



!!! tip

	- Acquire **Any Fishing Rods**
---
