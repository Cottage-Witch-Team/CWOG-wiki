
# Get a Fishing Rod

> It all starts here

---


While you can still fish with a plain old **Fishing Rod**{.aqua}, you can get much better results by crafting an advanced version, such as an **Iron**{.aqua}, **Gold**{.aqua}, or **Diamond Fishing Rod**{.aqua}. 

At first glance, they don't seem all that different, but **obtain a Tackle Box**{.pink} and you'll get more perspective on how much better these **Fishing Rods**{.aqua} can be.

Why use a rod when you can just dive in to hit 'em with your sword? Well, some fish **do not spawn in the world and can only be caught on a line**{.green}.
