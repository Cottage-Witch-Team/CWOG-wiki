
# Tackle Box

> Better than a tickle box

---


The **Tackle Box** allows you to modify your new **Fishing Rod**. Simply ==place the rod in the upper right slot==, and you can now add a **Hook**, some **Bait**, a **Bobber**, and **Fishing Line**. These items will determine the performance of your Fishing Rod.

The **Bobbers** and **Fishing Line** can be colored, and are *cosmetic changes only*.

**Bait** can either be caught (Minnows, Leeches) or made (Worms). Bait will draw more fish to your line! It has durability, but can be *Enchanted* to make it sturdier.
