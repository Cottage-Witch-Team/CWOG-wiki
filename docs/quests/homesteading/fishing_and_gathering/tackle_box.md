
# Tackle Box

> Better than a tickle box

---


The **Tackle Box**{.aqua} allows you to modify your new **Fishing Rod**{.aqua}. Simply **place the rod in the upper right slot**{.gold}, and you can now add a **Hook**{.aqua}, some **Bait**{.aqua}, a **Bobber**{.aqua}, and **Fishing Line**{.aqua}. These items will determine the performance of your Fishing Rod.

The **Bobbers**{.aqua} and **Fishing Line**{.aqua} can be colored, and are **cosmetic changes only**{.green}.

**Bait**{.aqua} can either be caught (Minnows, Leeches) or made (Worms). Bait will draw more fish to your line! It has durability, but can be **Enchanted**{.green} to make it sturdier.
