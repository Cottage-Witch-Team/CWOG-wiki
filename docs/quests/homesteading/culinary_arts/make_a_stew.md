
# Make a Stew

Now that you're ready to cook, let's try making some **Borscht**{.aqua}. Just like babshka used to make.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/borscht.png width:100 height:55 align:1 fit:true}
As you can see, it's a **Beetroot**{.aqua}-heavy **vegetable soup**{.green}, that doesn't have any **denial ingredients**{.green}.

Make sure you place &aat least 3 **Beetroot**{.aqua} and &aat least 1 **other vegetable**{.aqua} (or another **Beetroot**{.aqua}).

However, you can **add more than that**{.pink}! If you make it with **9 Beetroot**{.aqua}, it will give you **greater saturation**{.green}.

There is a **bug where the correct Saturation won't appear**{.pink} when you hover over the item. However, it will be correct when you eat it.

Stews can also **be placed down with &6shift + right click**{.green}!

Additionally, you can &aadd **Potions&a to your food**{.aqua} by including them in the 9 ingredients.
