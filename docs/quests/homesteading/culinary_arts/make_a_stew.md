
# Make a Stew


!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Borscht
---


Now that you're ready to cook, let's try making some **Borscht**. Just like babshka used to make.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/borscht.png width:100 height:55 align:1 fit:true}
As you can see, it's a **Beetroot**-heavy *vegetable soup*, that doesn't have any *denial ingredients*.

Make sure you place &aat least 3 **Beetroot** and &aat least 1 **other vegetable** (or another **Beetroot**).

However, you can ^^add more than that^^! If you make it with **9 Beetroot**, it will give you *greater saturation*.

There is a ^^bug where the correct Saturation won't appear^^ when you hover over the item. However, it will be correct when you eat it.

Stews can also *be placed down with &6shift + right click*!

Additionally, you can &aadd **Potions&a to your food** by including them in the 9 ingredients.
