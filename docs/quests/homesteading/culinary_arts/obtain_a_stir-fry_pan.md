
# Obtain a Stir-Fry Pan

To stir-fry things, you'll need two things: a **pan or stone**, and a **Gravy Boat**.

The Pan must be ==placed atop a heat source==. The **Gravy Boat** must be placed *directly next to the pan*. You can place it with ==shift right click==.
