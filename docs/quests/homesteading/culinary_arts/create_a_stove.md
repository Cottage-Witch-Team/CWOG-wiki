
# Create a Stove

> Steve's stove

---


A **Stove** is an incredibly useful tool. Thanks to your inherent magic, it is fueled at all times! You can ==place up to 6 food items== on the grill in order to cook them. They'll pop up into items when they're ready! You can &6place an upside-down **Basket** above it to collect them automatically.

The **Stove** can also be used as a heat source for the **Cooking Pot**, but if you do it that way, you cannot place items on the grill.
