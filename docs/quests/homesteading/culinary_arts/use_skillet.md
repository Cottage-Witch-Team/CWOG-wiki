
# Use Skillet

To complete this quest, you'll want to trigger the advancement by doing some real Cooking On The Go with your **skillet**{.aqua}. Simply **stand next to a heat source**{.gold} (**campfire**{.aqua}, **magma block**{.aqua}, **lava**{.aqua}, you name it!) while **holding the skillet in your main hand**{.gold} and **your raw ingredients in your off hand**{.gold}! Right click and hold to start sizzling.
