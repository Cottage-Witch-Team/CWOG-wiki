
# Use Skillet

To complete this quest, you'll want to trigger the advancement by doing some real Cooking On The Go with your **skillet**. Simply ==stand next to a heat source== (**campfire**, **magma block**, **lava**, you name it!) while ==holding the skillet in your main hand== and ==your raw ingredients in your off hand==! Right click and hold to start sizzling.
