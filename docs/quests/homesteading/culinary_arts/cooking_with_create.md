
# Cooking with Create

Did you know that you can &aautomate your cooking with **Create**{.blue}? You can!

Right click the **Cooking Guide**{.aqua} onto a **Blaze Burner**{.aqua} to **create a Blaze Stove**{.gold}.

Hold "w" to **ponder**{.gold} the **Cooking Guide**{.aqua} for more information.

Be sure that the **Cooking Guide**{.aqua} is outside of your **Akashic Tome**{.aqua}! **The book will be consumed**{.pink} by this process. Check the **tutorial quests**{.green} for info on removing a book from the **Akashic Tome**{.aqua}.
