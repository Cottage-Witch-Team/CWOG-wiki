
# Obtain a Cutting Board

> Back to the ol' cutting board

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Cutting Board
---


A **cutting board** will allow you to prepare advanced ingredients for your meals, such as **bacon** or **fish filets**. Simply ==place the item on the cutting board== and ==right click it== with your knife.

This isn't the only use for the **cutting board**! Try experimenting with items like **wooden logs** (using an **axe**) or even unwanted **leather armor** (with **shears**)!
