
# Obtain a Dolium

> The doldrums

---


The **Dolium**{.aqua} is used for **Resting Conversion**{.green}, which consists of placing your **ingredients and other items**{.green} inside and **letting them rest**{.gold}, for up to an **in-game day**{.green}.
Each recipe needs all ingredients, as well as a container to hold the result, and sometimes an additional item (such as **Anvils for Gravy Boats**{.green}). **The resting process will not begin without all components**{.pink}.
This can be sped up using the **Time in a Bottle**{.aqua}.

---



!!! tip "TASK" 

	- Acquire **Any Dolium**
