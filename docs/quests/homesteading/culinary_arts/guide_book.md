
# Guide Book

***Caupona*** is a cooking mod that employs a freeform style of recipe.
With most recipes, you will *maintain a ratio* of ingredients, such as ensuring that you have *"mostly beef"* and *"some vegetables"*, with *"no fish"*, and so on.
You can use **the guidebook** or ***JEI*** to learn the recipes.
{image:cottagewitch:textures/cauponajei.png width:100 height:80 align:1 fit:true}
When viewing **Stews and Soups**, ensure that you ==click these icons== to access their recipes.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/cauponarecipe.png width:100 height:90 align:1 fit:true}
A) This is the **Soup Base**. For example, for **Nail Soup**, you ==boil water==.
B) This is the *ratio of ingredients to liquid*. For example, 3 or more ingredients to 1 bucket/4 bowls of liquid.
C) This is the *output*, or what the recipe will make.
D) These are ingredients which *must not be included*.
E and F) These are the *ratio of items to each other*, for example, a majority of **beef** and at least one **vegetable**.

Now that you know how to read the recipes, let's make some!
