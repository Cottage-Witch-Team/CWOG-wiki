
# The Spice of Life

> (psst, it's variety)

---


One feature of this modpack is that *eating a variety of foods grants you extra hearts*! Make sure to ==maintain a wide diet==, as these bonus hearts will diminish if you find yourself living off a stack of egg sandwiches or Everlasting Beef.

You can see which foods you've eaten by checking your **Food Book**, which is inside your **Akashic Tome** and can be accessed by ==right clicking the Tome and then selecting the Food Book==.

You can also find what you've yet to eat by searching "yet" in the right side of your inventory screen (***JEI***).
