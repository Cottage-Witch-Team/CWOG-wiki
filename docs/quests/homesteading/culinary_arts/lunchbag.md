
# Lunchbag

> Bring lunch with you!

---


A **lunchbag**, upgradeable to an iron or gold lunchbox, is a handy-dandy way to carry a variety of foods with you wherever you go. You can *eat the lunchbox directly* for a randomised snack from its interior- an excellent way to easily *increase your food variety score*, which increases your hearts!

A word of warning... don't trust seagulls. They'll swoop down and *steal food right out of your inventory*; even your whole lunchbox! 

^^Caution: entering Creative Mode with the lunchbox in your inventory will cause its contents to be wiped.^^
