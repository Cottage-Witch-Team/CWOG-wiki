
# A Different Dragon Egg

Once you've obtained a **Dragon Egg**, you can use them to craft a **Nourishing Dragon Egg**.

This item can only be cut with the *extreme sharpness* of a **Nether Star** on a **Cutting Board**. Cutting open the egg will give you *several ingredients* needed for high-level meals.
