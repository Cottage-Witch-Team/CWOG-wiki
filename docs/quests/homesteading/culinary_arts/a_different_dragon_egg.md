
# A Different Dragon Egg

Once you've obtained a **Dragon Egg**{.aqua}, you can use them to craft a **Nourishing Dragon Egg**{.aqua}.

This item can only be cut with the **extreme sharpness**{.green} of a **Nether Star**{.aqua} on a **Cutting Board**{.aqua}. Cutting open the egg will give you **several ingredients**{.green} needed for high-level meals.
