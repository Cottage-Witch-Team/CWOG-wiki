
# Obtain a Kettle

> Don't let it call your cooking pot black.

---


The **Kettle** must be ==placed on a heat source== in order to brew tea. You can use a **campfire**, **magma block**, or other source of warmth.

Use a **water bucket** or 3 **water bottles** to fill the **kettle** with water by ==right clicking them== on the block. Then, ==right click== and place your brewing ingredients inside!
