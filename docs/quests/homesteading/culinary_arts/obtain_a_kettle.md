
# Obtain a Kettle

> Don't let it call your cooking pot black.

---


The **Kettle**{.aqua} must be **placed on a heat source**{.gold} in order to brew tea. You can use a **campfire**{.aqua}, **magma block**{.aqua}, or other source of warmth.

Use a **water bucket**{.aqua} or 3 **water bottles**{.aqua} to fill the **kettle**{.aqua} with water by **right clicking them**{.gold} on the block. Then, **right click**{.gold} and place your brewing ingredients inside!
