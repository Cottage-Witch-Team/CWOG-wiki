
# A Hat In A Bag?

> It Came From The Sea

---
Hats! Who doesn't live the simple pleasure of a Nice Hat? &lSimple Hats&r is just that: hats, nothing else. These are exclusively equipped in your Head curio slot, and can &aonly be found through fishing&r (or a lucky neptunian chest).

Each regular hatbag contains one hat of corresponding rarity: Common, &eUncommon&r, &bRare&r, and &dEpic&r! 

You also have a chance to find &bseasonal exclusives&r: Spring, Summer, Fall and Winter. These are only available for a 2-week period each (northern hemisphere) season! During those periods you will have a 100% chance of getting a &bseasonal hat&r with your regular hat!
