
# Overweight Crops

> Everyone loves a chubby carrot

---


With this mod, crops have a small chance of spawning *giant crops*, similar to Stardew Valley. This is most likely to * happen to fully grown crops exposed to Moonlight*.

Break them with a **Hoe** to get significantly more regular crops than usual. Break them with an **Axe** or your hand to obtain the Crop Block itself. You can then *Cook* them, *Peel* them, or place them on a **Cutting Board** to gather the crops from them.

And you even have a chance of Overweight Crops when using **Planters**!

**Rich Farmland** has an increased chance of spawning Overweight Crops.
