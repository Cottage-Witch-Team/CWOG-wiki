
# Craft a Cart

> Item and animal transport

---


These **Carts**{.aqua} are useful for transporting **items and entities**{.green}. **Press "r"**{.gold} by default when either mounted or on foot to **haul the cart**{.green}. Press R again to set it down.

The **Supply Cart**{.aqua} is fairly self-explanatory. **Shift right click the cart**{.gold} to access its inventory, and **load it up with items**{.green}! You can then transport those items over land. However, **breaking the cart**{.green} will drop its inventory.

The **Animal Cart**{.aqua} will help you **transport entities**{.green}! **Right click the cart item**{.gold} onto the creature you want to move. Then, haul as normal. **Break the cart**{.green} to free the animal.
