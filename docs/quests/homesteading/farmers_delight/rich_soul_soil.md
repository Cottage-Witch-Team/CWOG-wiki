
# Rich Soul Soil

Much like its browner brother, **Rich Soul Soil**{.aqua} is formed of **Soil Compost**{.aqua} that has been **left adjacent to Lava**{.green}.
You can use it to grow **Nether Crops**{.aqua} more quickly!
