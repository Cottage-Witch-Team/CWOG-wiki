
# Even Better Nexus Upgrades

> Advanced Card

---
In addition to the &bCarved Certus Tablets&r, you can also craft &aadvanced upgrades&r using &bFluix Tablets&r.

Fluix as a crystal has certain properties - a pseudo-sentience, if you will. In order to perform tasks which require some "smart" functions, it's a much better choice than plain &bCertus&r.
