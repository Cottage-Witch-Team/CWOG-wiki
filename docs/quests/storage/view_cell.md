Place the &bGlyphstone&r in the &6upper right slot&r to alter its contents. Then, you can insert ghosts of items from &ayour inventory or even &lJEI&r&r by clicking and dragging the item into its inventory.

Once you have finished configuring the glyphstone, you can &6place the &bDiscernment Glyphstone&r into the upper right slots of your &aNexus Aperture&r.
