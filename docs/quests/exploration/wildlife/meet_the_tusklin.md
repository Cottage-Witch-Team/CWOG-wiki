
# Meet the Tusklin

> A neutral but defensive beast

---


The **Tusklin**{.yellow}, found in snowy biomes, can be saddled and ridden wild, but will buck the player off after a while. They can be bred with **Brown Mushrooms**{.aqua}, but their young will still be mostly feral.
