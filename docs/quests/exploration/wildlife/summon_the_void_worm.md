
# Summon the Void Worm

> A multi-phase boss fight

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Mysterious Worm
---


The **Void Worm** is a boss fight which can be summoned by throwing a **Mysterious Worm** into the void in ^^The End^^. He summons crystalline projectiles, has a devastating bite, and is capable of opening portals.
When defeated, it drops its **Eyes** and **Beak**. These can be crafted into a **Dimensional Carver**: a pickaxe which can mine a temporary portal through space and to the player's spawn point, and the **Void Worm Effigy**: a trophy that, when provided with redstone signal, chomps down on whatever is in front of it.
