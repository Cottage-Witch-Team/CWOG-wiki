
# Kill Axolotl Target

Axolotls are cute lil guys, but they're also *strong* and *vicious*. If you tame one, it will *fight alongside you* should you decide to take on an underwater threat.
They'll also sometimes *give you Regeneration*, and once they do so, you'll know that you are ^^best friends 5ever^^.
