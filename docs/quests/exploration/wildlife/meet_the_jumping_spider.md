
# Meet The Jumping Spider!

> You Aren't So Scary, Are Ya?

---
Oh wow, a &eJumping Spider&r! Tame it with &bdragonfly wings&r and it will protect you. They also drop &bspider silk&r, which can be used to craft the &bsilk leash&r and &bsilk grappling hook&r.

Note: dragonflies from this mod do not spawn in the world, due to their laggy AI. However, you can find them- and many other creepy crawlies- in the &bInsectoid Gatepearl&r.
