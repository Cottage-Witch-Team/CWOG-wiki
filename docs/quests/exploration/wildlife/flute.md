
# Flute

> This is literally so adorable

---


This delightful item not only *plays a lovely tune* when you ==hold right click while holding it==, but it also *summons all non-sitting pets nearby* to your location.
You can optionally bind your flute to a specific pet by ==shift right clicking== on the animal with the flute.
