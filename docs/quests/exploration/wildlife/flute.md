
# Flute

> This is literally so adorable

---


This delightful item not only **plays a lovely tune**{.green} when you **hold right click while holding it**{.gold}, but it also **summons all non-sitting pets nearby**{.green} to your location.
You can optionally bind your flute to a specific pet by **shift right clicking**{.gold} on the animal with the flute.
