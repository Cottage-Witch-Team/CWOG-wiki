
# Froststalker Helmet


!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Froststalker Helmet
---


The **Froststalker** helmet is worn to convince **Froststalkers** that the player is one of them.
It must be crafted from one of their horns. Once it is worn, you can ==breed them with pork== - but be warned, taking off the helmet will ^^make the babies hostile as well^^.
