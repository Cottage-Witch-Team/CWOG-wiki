
# Meet the Slabfish

The **Slabfish**{.yellow} is a rectangular entity which can be **tamed**{.green} with **Tropical Fish**{.aqua}.
Once tamed, you can **right click**{.gold} to **give them a sweater**{.green} by equipping them with **Wool**{.aqua}. They can also help you carry items if you &agive them a **Chest**{.aqua}.
You can also **dye the chest backpack**{.green} by placing a dye in that slot.
**Slabfish**{.yellow} will toggle sitting or standing if you **shift right click**{.gold} them.
However, they will &donly follow you when you're holding **Tropical Fish**{.aqua}.


Try **breeding them together in different biomes**{.gold}! There's &omany types&r of slabfish.
