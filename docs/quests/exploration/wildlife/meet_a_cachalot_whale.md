The *Cachalot whale*, more commonly called Sperm whale, is a massive creature that dwells in *Lukewarm, Cold, or Deep Oceans*.
Sometimes they *become beached* during storms, and if you were to ==push one back out to sea== it may thank you with a gift of **Ambergris**.
It likes to prey on **Giant Squid**, and will sometimes drop one of its teeth during a hunt.
