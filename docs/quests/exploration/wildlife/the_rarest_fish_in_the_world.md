
# The Rarest Fish in the World

> Speak of the devil...

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Meet Devils Hole Pupfish
---


The *Devil's Hole Pupfish* is the rarest being in the world, spawning in only a single chunk - EVER - in any given world.
Find one with a **Strange Fish Locator**. Or, you know, get extremely lucky.
The ponds they spawn in are usually underground. These fish love to nibble on **Moss Blocks**, and will sometimes breed after doing so!
