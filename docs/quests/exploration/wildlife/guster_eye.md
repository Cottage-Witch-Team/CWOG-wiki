
# Guster Eye

The **Guster** is an elemental spirit which can appear during storms. It will throw nearby entities or items up into the air, and are *resistant to arrows*.
Once defeated, they can drop their **Eyes**, which can be used either to make a **Pocket of Sand**, to throw in the face of your enemies - or the **Gustmaker**, a block which acts as a fan when provided with a Redstone signal.

---



!!! tip

	- Acquire **Guster Eye**
