
# Creeper Collector

> Kill one of each creeper type

---


Although they may not all be hostile, it would still be pretty cool if you killed one of every type of **Creeper**. It's like a world tour, but with murder!

As a reward, here's a **particularly rare creeper** that doesn't spawn in the world... maybe keep it in an aquarium as a trophy?

Note: As some creepers may be harder to find than others, you may choose to *tackle them all at once* using a **Creeper Gate Pearl**. But be warned- ^^if a single creeper blows up^^, the gate will collapse. Reach the final wave to meet and defeat every creeper!
