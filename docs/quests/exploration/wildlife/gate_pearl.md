
# Gate Pearl

A **Gate Pearl**{.aqua} is a contained ritual which will **summon waves of enemies**{.green}, and grant **rewards**{.aqua} for completing each wave, and for defeating the challenge.
This item **summons &eFarseers**{.green}, a long-forgotten race of multidimensional spirits which inhabit metallic bodies. These entities **are very powerful**{.green} and can easily kill even the most powerful witch with a single **deadly stare**{.green}.
**Press shift**{.gold} while hovering over the Pearl in your inventory to view information about the **combat challenge**{.green}. Good luck!
