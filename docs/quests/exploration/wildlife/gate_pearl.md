
# Gate Pearl

A **Gate Pearl** is a contained ritual which will *summon waves of enemies*, and grant **rewards** for completing each wave, and for defeating the challenge.
This item *summons &eFarseers*, a long-forgotten race of multidimensional spirits which inhabit metallic bodies. These entities *are very powerful* and can easily kill even the most powerful witch with a single *deadly stare*.
==Press shift== while hovering over the Pearl in your inventory to view information about the *combat challenge*. Good luck!
