
# Rainbow Jelly

> Jellyfish in the sky~ i can go twice as high~

---


Defeat a **Rainbow Jelly** to obtain a drop which can craft **Rainbow Glass**. You can also ==right click it onto an entity== to paint that entity rainbow. Wipe them clean with a sponge, if you wish.
