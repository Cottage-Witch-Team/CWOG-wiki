
# Meet the Capuchin Monkey

> Not to be confused with the delicious cappuchino monkey

---


The **Capuchin Monkey**{.yellow} is a friendly beast which can be tamed with **Bananas**{.aqua}, which drop from Jungle Tree Leaves. They can be bred with **Maggots**{.aqua}, which drop from **Flies**{.yellow}, and healed with **Eggs**{.aqua}.
These monkeys can also be told to **perch on your shoulder**{.green}, where they will throw items at your enemies. If you find a rare **Ancient Dart**{.aqua}, you may give it to them to grant them extra damage.
