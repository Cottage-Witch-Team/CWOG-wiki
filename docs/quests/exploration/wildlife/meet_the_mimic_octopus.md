
# Meet the Mimic Octopus

> Much nicer than the other kind of mimic

---


The **Mimic Octopus**{.yellow} is an incredible being that is found in **Warm Oceans**{.green}. They can be tamed with **Lobster Tails**{.aqua}, at which point they will follow you and transform to try to scare off any enemies you may encounter.
Give your Octopus an **Ink Sac**{.aqua} to toggle whether it can randomly change color.
Give it some **Prismarine Shards**{.aqua} to give it a Guardian-like appearance.
Give it a **Pufferfish**{.aqua} if you'd like it to look like one of those, and hmm. I wonder what happens if you give one **Gunpowder**{.aqua}?
Feed it some **Mimicream**{.aqua} to teach it the abilities of whatever creature you've given it!
