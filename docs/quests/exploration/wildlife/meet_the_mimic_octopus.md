
# Meet the Mimic Octopus

> Much nicer than the other kind of mimic

---


The **Mimic Octopus** is an incredible being that is found in *Warm Oceans*. They can be tamed with **Lobster Tails**, at which point they will follow you and transform to try to scare off any enemies you may encounter.
Give your Octopus an **Ink Sac** to toggle whether it can randomly change color.
Give it some **Prismarine Shards** to give it a Guardian-like appearance.
Give it a **Pufferfish** if you'd like it to look like one of those, and hmm. I wonder what happens if you give one **Gunpowder**?
Feed it some **Mimicream** to teach it the abilities of whatever creature you've given it!
