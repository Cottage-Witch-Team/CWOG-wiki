
# Wayward Lantern

The **Wayward Lantern** will allow any lost pets to *find their way home*. Place it near your base, and if you should lose your pet while exploring, watch for them there.
If you *lose your pet in another dimension*, you will need to ==place a Lantern in that dimension== to get them back.

In ^^Multiplayer^^, your pets will respawn at the *closest Lantern*, so be careful in other people's areas.
