
# Meet the Kākāpō

> The world's largest flightless parrot

---


These loveable fellows may not do much besides grunt and dance and sit pretty, but they happen to be my **favourite animal**{.pink}, and I require nothing more than their existence.

**Kākāpō**{.yellow}, often called owl parrots, are nocturnal flightless parrots endemic to **New Zealand**{.red}. These chubsters are some of the longest living birds on the planet- capable of living up to 100 years! Though they were **nearly wiped out**{.pink} by the introduction of mammals to the island, there are many **conservation efforts**{.green} on nearby islands and they have recently been **successfully reintroduced**{.green} to the **Sanctuary Mountain Maungatautari**{.red} on the mainland. 

There are currently only **247 living Kākāpō in existence**{.pink}. As such, they have been made **incredibly scarce**{.green} in the modpack here as well- being found only in the **Guiana Shield**{.red} biome. To increase your chances of finding this bird, **try using the**{.gold} **Summoning Ritual**{.aqua} **from**{.gold} **Ars Nouveau**{.glow} in a **Guiana Shield**{.red}.

If you are interested in the conservation of these precious creatures, you can learn more here: &9www.doc.govt.nz/kakapo-donate&r
