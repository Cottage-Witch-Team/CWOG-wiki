
# Hatching the Duck

Approach this quest with caution, dear witches. **Ducks** may seem cute and innocent, ^^but they are not^^.
In the early days of the world, **Ducks** swarmed the world, roving over rivers and quacking incessantly. It led to the *Great Duck War*, in which witches wished wistfully for success against their foes. And killed a lot of ducks.
Now, you may ==trade in 16 chicken eggs== in order to receive *a single Duck*. But beware of causing another *Duck War*!!
