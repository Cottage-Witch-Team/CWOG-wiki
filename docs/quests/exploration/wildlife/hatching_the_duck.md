
# Hatching the Duck

Approach this quest with caution, dear witches. **Ducks**{.yellow} may seem cute and innocent, **but they are not**{.pink}.
In the early days of the world, **Ducks**{.yellow} swarmed the world, roving over rivers and quacking incessantly. It led to the **Great Duck War**{.green}, in which witches wished wistfully for success against their foes. And killed a lot of ducks.
Now, you may **trade in 16 chicken eggs**{.gold} in order to receive **a single Duck**{.green}. But beware of causing another **Duck War**{.green}!!
