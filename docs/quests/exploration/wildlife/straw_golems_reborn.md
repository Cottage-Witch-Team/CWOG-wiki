
# Straw Golems: Reborn

> Wheat is friends, not food!

---


Do you have lots of **pumpkins and melons**{.aqua} that need farming? A **Straw Golem**{.yellow} may just be the buddy for you! These little guys will **harvest fresh gourds and melons**{.green} and **drop them at the nearest carved pumpkin**{.green}.* Why do they love pumpkins so much? &oDon't ask so many questions.&r

While villages of wild Straw Golems may be found in **Autumnal Valleys**{.red}, you can build your own! Simply **place a carved pumpkin on top of a bale of hay**{.gold}. Careful not to startle them... they spook at the &odrop of a hat!&r

*These fellas can be a little imprecise... to make the most of your farm, we suggest using an** item collector**{.aqua} to pick up the drops, and **rich soil farmland**{.aqua} so they dont break your plants!

**While the **Straw Golem mod**{.glow} &dwas found to be too buggy to keep in the pack, we have reskinned Pumpkin Wardens to wear their texture so we can keep our little friends!**{.pink}
