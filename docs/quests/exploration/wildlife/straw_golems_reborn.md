
# Straw Golems: Reborn

> Wheat is friends, not food!

---


Do you have lots of **pumpkins and melons** that need farming? A **Straw Golem** may just be the buddy for you! These little guys will *harvest fresh gourds and melons* and *drop them at the nearest carved pumpkin*.* Why do they love pumpkins so much? &oDon't ask so many questions.&r

While villages of wild Straw Golems may be found in *Autumnal Valleys*, you can build your own! Simply ==place a carved pumpkin on top of a bale of hay==. Careful not to startle them... they spook at the &odrop of a hat!&r

*These fellas can be a little imprecise... to make the most of your farm, we suggest using an** item collector** to pick up the drops, and **rich soil farmland** so they dont break your plants!

^^While the ***Straw Golem mod*** &dwas found to be too buggy to keep in the pack, we have reskinned Pumpkin Wardens to wear their texture so we can keep our little friends!^^
