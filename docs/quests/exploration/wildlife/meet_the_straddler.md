
# Meet the Straddler

> Just gonna throw my baby at you real quick

---


The **Straddler** is a bizarre and hostile creature who will throw their young at you to do damage. For the most part, defeating them yields **Basalt**, however they can rarely drop **Straddlite**.
**Straddlite** is a material which can be crafted into a Straddleboard, an enchantable board which can *surf quickly across lava*. This is an improvement over the **Obsidian Boat** due to its speed, enchantability, and ability to jump!
And, as a side note, the **Stradpoles** can be nabbed in a bucket and also *survive in water*.
