
# Deed Of Ownership


# Task:

- Acquire Deed Of Ownership
---

The **Deed of Ownership** is an item used in *multiplayer*. You can ==right click it on an animal== and then give the **Deed** to a friend. When they use it, the pet will now be linked to them, *as if they had tamed it themself*.

You can learn that the **Deed of Ownership** is only obtainable by *trading with an animal tamer villager* by ==left clicking== on the **Deed of Ownership** in ***JEI***
