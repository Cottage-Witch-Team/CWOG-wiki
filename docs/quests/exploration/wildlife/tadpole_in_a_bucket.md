
# Tadpole In A Bucket

**Tadpoles**, otherwise known as "the forbidden boba," are cute lil wiggly fellas who are particularly susceptible to **Axolotls**.
They're also susceptible to **Buckets**, and if you should capture one in such a way, then you've completed this quest.
