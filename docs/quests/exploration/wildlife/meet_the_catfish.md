
# Meet the Catfish

> A hungry fellow

---


**Catfish**, which come in three different size categories, will habitually *swallow up dropped items or smaller entities* they come across.
However, if they take any damage - or encounter a sea pickle, which they loathe - *they will eject the contents of their stomachs*.
One can ==lure them in== with a **Sea Lantern**, and grab them with a **Bucket**.
