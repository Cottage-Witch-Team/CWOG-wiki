
# Meet the Catfish

> A hungry fellow

---


**Catfish**{.yellow}, which come in three different size categories, will habitually **swallow up dropped items or smaller entities**{.green} they come across.
However, if they take any damage - or encounter a sea pickle, which they loathe - **they will eject the contents of their stomachs**{.green}.
One can **lure them in**{.gold} with a **Sea Lantern**{.aqua}, and grab them with a **Bucket**{.aqua}.
