
# Meet a Mungus

> Pretty sus ngl

---


The **Mungus** can be found in Mushroom biomes and is a living meme. It drops **Mungal Spores** periodically every few days, and this item can be used to breed it.
**Mushrooms** can be placed upon it, at which point it will target that same type of mushroom and attempt to spawn more of it, or turn it to its giant variant.
A **Mungus** which has 5 mushrooms on its back will explode when slain, changing the small nearby area to a Mushroom Fields.
