
# The Overcast Columns

According to the ancient texts:

The *Overcast Columns* are a vast, uninhabited wasteland. They are composed of multiple amber columns, which have a biome-exclusive **Crystallized Amber** block in each of them. Overcast Columns also have multiple **Geysers** scattered throughout the terrain, which is composed of **Gloomslate** and **Gloom Sculk**. Nothing is known to live here due to the *hostile and barren nature* of the environment.
