
# Craft a Warden Helmet

In order to brave the **Otherside**{.red}, you will want to be very geared up in general, but the piece of armor you'll need most is the **Warden Helmet**{.aqua}.

Aside from its defense and armor toughness, it grants **immunity to Darkness**{.aqua}, allowing you to see.
