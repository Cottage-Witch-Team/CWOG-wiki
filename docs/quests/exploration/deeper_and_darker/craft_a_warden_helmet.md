
# Craft a Warden Helmet

In order to brave the *Otherside*, you will want to be very geared up in general, but the piece of armor you'll need most is the **Warden Helmet**.

Aside from its defense and armor toughness, it grants **immunity to Darkness**, allowing you to see.

---



!!! tip

	- Acquire **Warden Helmet**
