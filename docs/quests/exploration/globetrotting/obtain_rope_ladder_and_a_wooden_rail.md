
# Obtain Rope Ladder and a Wooden Rail

> It’s probably strong enough, right?

---


Craft yourself some **Rope Ladders**{.aqua} and **Wooden Rails**{.aqua} to carefully traverse difficult terrain - easy to make, easy to place, and able to **chain across air gaps**{.green} without support blocks below them. Vertical and Horizontal travel has never been so handy! Both can be placed similarly to **Scaffolding**{.aqua}- **right click one onto another**{.gold} and they’ll build in the direction you face. You can even **pull your rope ladder up**{.green} behind you by right clicking the top piece! 

---



!!! tip "TASK" 
	### Acquire :
	- **Rope Ladder**
	- **Wooden Rail**
