
# Obtain a Sharestone

**Sharestones** allow for travel *only within their colored network*. From a Red Sharestone, you can teleport to any other Red Sharestone. However, you cannot do so from an Orange Sharestone.
This makes them very useful for having private or separate networks of travel. They cannot be accessed from **Warp Stones** or any **Scrolls**.
