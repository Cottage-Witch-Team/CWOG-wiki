
# Obtain Magnetite

> I Choose You, Magnetite!

---
&bMagnetite&r generates in small clusters underground, surrounded by lava and deepslate iron, &aencased in a shell of obsidian&r. Harvest this rare ore with your Pickaxe to get magnetite chunks… or for &bMagnetite ore&r, use a Hammer + Chisel (or &asilk touch&r). Both of these items will help you discover some strangely attractive new crafting recipes!
