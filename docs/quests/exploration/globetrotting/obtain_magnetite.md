
# Obtain Magnetite

> I choose you, magnetite!

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Magnetite
---


**Magnetite** generates in small clusters underground, surrounded by lava and deepslate iron, *encased in a shell of obsidian*. Harvest this rare ore with your Pickaxe to get magnetite chunks… or for **Magnetite ore**, use a Hammer + Chisel (or *silk touch*). Both of these items will help you discover some strangely attractive new crafting recipes!
