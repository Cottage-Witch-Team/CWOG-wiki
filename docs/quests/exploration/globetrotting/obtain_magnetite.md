
# Obtain Magnetite

> I choose you, magnetite!

---


**Magnetite**{.aqua} generates in small clusters underground, surrounded by lava and deepslate iron, **encased in a shell of obsidian**{.green}. Harvest this rare ore with your Pickaxe to get magnetite chunks… or for **Magnetite ore**{.aqua}, use a Hammer + Chisel (or **silk touch**{.green}). Both of these items will help you discover some strangely attractive new crafting recipes!
