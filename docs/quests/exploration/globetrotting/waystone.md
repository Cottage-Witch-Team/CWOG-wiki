
# Waystone

**Waystones**{.aqua} are a method for fast travel between places in the world. Once a Waystone has been **activated**{.green}, it can be ported to from any other Waystone, or using other methods.
**Waystones**{.aqua} generate naturally in **Villages**{.pink}, but they can also be crafted.
