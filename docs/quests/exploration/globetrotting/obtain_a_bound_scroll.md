
# Obtain a Bound Scroll

The **Bound Scroll**{.aqua} is a single-use item which can teleport to you **a Waystone to which it is bound**{.green}. Simply **crouch and right click**{.gold} the Waystone to bind the scroll. Each scroll must be bound individually. However, you can craft **Return Scrolls**{.aqua} and bind an entire stack at once.
Simply **hold right click with it in your hand**{.gold} in order to activate it. If you take damage or let go while it is charging, **the warp will cancel**{.pink}.
