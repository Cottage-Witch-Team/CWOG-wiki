
# Nexus

**Deep underground**{.green} is the structure known as **The Nexus**{.red}. This carved deepslate monstrosity contains broken portals to other worlds, such as the **Nether**{.red} and **The Twilight Forest**{.red}.
These gateways are broken and in need of mending. Unfortunately it seems **most are broken irreparably**{.green}, and those worlds cannot be visited.
