
# Nexus


!!! tip "Task:"

	- Unlock Nexus
---


*Deep underground* is the structure known as *The Nexus*. This carved deepslate monstrosity contains broken portals to other worlds, such as the *Nether* and *The Twilight Forest*.
These gateways are broken and in need of mending. Unfortunately it seems *most are broken irreparably*, and those worlds cannot be visited.
