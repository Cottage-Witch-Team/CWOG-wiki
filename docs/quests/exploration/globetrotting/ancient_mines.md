
# Ancient Mines


!!! tip "Task:"

	- Unlock Ancient Mines
---


The *Ancient Mines* been to be seen to be believed. A massive underground chasm, once inhabited by those who delved too greedily and too deep. Beware what now lives there.
^^CW: Giant Cave Centipedes^^. Although they are disabled in most of the modpack, this structure has a spawner for them. Be prepared, should you go inside.
