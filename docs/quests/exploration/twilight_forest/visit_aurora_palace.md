
# Visit: Aurora Palace

> There's an elsa joke in here somewhere

---


The **Snow Queen**{.yellow} is found at the top of the **Aurora Palace**{.pink}, a treacherous mansion filled with icy monsters and slippery parkour.
The Queen herself has three phases, and cannot be damaged through her **Ice Throne**{.green}, so only her upper body can be targeted.
When she is defeated, you have completed one of three tasks to gain access to **The Highlands**{.pink}.
