
# Obtain a Portal Key

> Throw it in a limpid pool and then back away

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Diamond
---


^^The Twilight Forest^^ is a night-ridden dimension filled with mysteries and odd creatures both friendly and hostile. Freely traveling the dimension is difficult - you must conquer each challenge in turn in order to traverse.
To *make the portal*, you must ==dig a 3x3x1 (or 2x2x1) hole and fill it with water==. Then, ==surround the pond with flowers or mushrooms==. Finally, ==throw a Diamond into the pool==. Beware, opening a portal can be dangerous!
