
# Find a Naga Courtyard

> Nag your local naga

---

# Task:

- Find Naga Courtyard
---

The first boss you must face is the block-destroying **Naga**. It is a long serpent who slowly shrinks in length as you damage him.
If the player flees the courtyard, the Naga will return to the center.
Once he's defeated, ==be sure to loot the chest that he spawns==, as you will need the **Naga Trophy** inside of it.
