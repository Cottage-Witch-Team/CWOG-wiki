
# Moonworm Queen

> You might want to sit down for this one

---


**The Moonworm Queen** is an...item...that is filled with Moonworms. When you ==right click her==, they will be dispensed - either directly onto a surface, or shot like an arrow. The moonworms serve no purpose other than *as a light source*. You can replenish the Queen by crafting her with **Torchberries**, and get...even more worms. Yay.
