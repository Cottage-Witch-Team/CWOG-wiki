
# Reach the Twilight Forest

> Entering a crepuscular zone

---


Welcome to the Twilight Forest! This dimension can be dangerous to navigate, although it is convieniently laid out with its **major landmarks in a grid**{.green}.
The first thing you will want is a **Map**{.aqua}, and for that, you'll need to find a **Raven**{.yellow}.
And remember! **If you get lost or wander into an inaccessible zone, look for Kobolds**{.gold}. They will be carrying the journal of previous adventurers, which will clue you in to your next goal.
