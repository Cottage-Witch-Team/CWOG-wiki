
# Obtain the Peacock Feather Fan

> Ostentatious but very useful

---


The **Peacock Feather Fan**{.aqua} is an item which sends out a mighty gust of wind when **right clicked**{.gold}.
You can use this to gain an advantage in combat, or even to give yourself a boost in midair, when jumping or using **Elytra**{.aqua}!

---



!!! tip "TASK" 

	- Acquire **Peacock Feather Fan**
