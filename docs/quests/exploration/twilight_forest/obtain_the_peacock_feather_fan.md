
# Obtain the Peacock Feather Fan

> Ostentatious but very useful

---


The **Peacock Feather Fan** is an item which sends out a mighty gust of wind when ==right clicked==.
You can use this to gain an advantage in combat, or even to give yourself a boost in midair, when jumping or using **Elytra**!

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Peacock Feather Fan
