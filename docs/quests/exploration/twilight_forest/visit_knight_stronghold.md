
# Visit: Knight Stronghold

> Better bring some extra torches

---

# Task:

- Find Knight Stronghold
---

Located in the gloomy depths of the ^^Dark Forest^^, the Knight Stronghold is infested with **Goblins**, and you cannot enter without one of the **Trophies** from the previous bosses.
You can use **Maze Maps** here, but the structures are so massive that you may need a number of them.
It is recommended to ==mark your path with torches== to ensure that you don't double back by mistake.
The end of this dungeon contains a fight against the **Knight Phantoms**. These will not follow you out of the room, and can swarm you if you simply charge in. It is recommended to ==retreat out to the hallway periodically if healing is needed==.
Once you defeat them, you can now challenge the ^^Dark Tower^^.
