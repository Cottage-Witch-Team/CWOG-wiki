
# Visit: Lich Tower

> Where my liches at?

---


Inside the **Lich Tower**{.pink} is the undead noble of the Twilight Forest. His **Castle**{.green} is fraught with mobs, but also filled with loot.
It is recommended to be well-geared before attempting this structure.
At the top of the tower, you will find the **Lich**{.yellow}, who is surrounded by a shield which protects him. **He must be defeated first with his own magic**{.gold}, then martial might can finish the fight.
And beware of the copies he summons during the fight!
Defeating the **Lich**{.yellow} grants you access to the **Yeti's Lair**{.pink}, the **Dark Forest**{.pink}, and the **Swamp**{.pink}.
