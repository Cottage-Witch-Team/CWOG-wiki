
# Visit: Lich Tower

> Where my liches at?

---


Inside the ^^Lich Tower^^ is the undead noble of the Twilight Forest. His *Castle* is fraught with mobs, but also filled with loot.
It is recommended to be well-geared before attempting this structure.
At the top of the tower, you will find the **Lich**, who is surrounded by a shield which protects him. ==He must be defeated first with his own magic==, then martial might can finish the fight.
And beware of the copies he summons during the fight!
Defeating the **Lich** grants you access to the ^^Yeti's Lair^^, the ^^Dark Forest^^, and the ^^Swamp^^.
