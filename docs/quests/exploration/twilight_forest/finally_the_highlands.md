
# Finally: The Highlands!

> You did it!

---


You've finally reached the Highlands! This is going to sound suspicious, but hear me out: you need to find some **Magic Beans**{.aqua}.
You can obtain some either by killing **Trolls**{.yellow} underground, or by looting their obsidian-bound Caches.
Then, find a large patch of **Uberous Soil**{.aqua}, above which the **Giants**{.yellow} live. You'll need to defeat their Miner to obtain his **Giant Pickaxe**{.aqua}.
Then, you can **return underground and use the Giant Pick**{.gold} to break open the large Obsidian Vault. It will take a while.
