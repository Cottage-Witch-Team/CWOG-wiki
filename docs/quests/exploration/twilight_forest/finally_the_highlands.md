
# Finally: The Highlands!

> You did it!

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Visit Highlands
---


You've finally reached the Highlands! This is going to sound suspicious, but hear me out: you need to find some **Magic Beans**.
You can obtain some either by killing **Trolls** underground, or by looting their obsidian-bound Caches.
Then, find a large patch of **Uberous Soil**, above which the **Giants** live. You'll need to defeat their Miner to obtain his **Giant Pickaxe**.
Then, you can ==return underground and use the Giant Pick== to break open the large Obsidian Vault. It will take a while.
