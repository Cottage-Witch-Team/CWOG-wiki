> I ain't afraid of no ghast

# Task:

- Find Dark Tower
---

In the Center of the ^^Dark Forest^^, the Dark Tower can be found. It is a massive structure, filled with monsters, and ruled over by a deadly **Ur-Ghast**.
There are four smaller turrets, each containing a **Tower Key**. These keys are used to unlock the central tower. There are many challenges here to undergo, so be prepared for a long expedition.
To defeat the **Ur-Ghast**, you must defeat his **Carminite Ghastlings** in the nearby rooms, near the Ghast Traps. Then and when the beast draws near, activate the trap.
The Ur-Ghast is one of the 3 bosses you must defeat to access the ^^Highlands^^.
