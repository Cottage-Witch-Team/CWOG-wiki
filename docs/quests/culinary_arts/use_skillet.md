
# Use Skillet



---
To complete this quest, you'll want to trigger the advancement by doing some real Cooking On The Go with your &bskillet&r. Simply &6stand next to a heat source&r (&bcampfire&r, &bmagma block&r, &blava&r, you name it!) while &6holding the skillet in your main hand&r and &6your raw ingredients in your off hand&r! Right click and hold to start sizzling.
