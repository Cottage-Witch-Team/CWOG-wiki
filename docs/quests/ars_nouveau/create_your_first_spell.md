
# Create Your First Spell

> Tick The Box When You're Ready

---
To make a spell, you must choose the &aForm&r (touch, self, or projectile) and the &aEffect&r (harm or break). These are called &bGlyphs&r, and you'll unlock more as you go.

For example, you could &6choose Projectile and Break&r to have a remote way of breaking blocks. Input the &aspell name&r in the bottom left, and click &dCreate&r in the bottom right. It will save to your &afirst spell slot&r (the 10 tabs on the right are your &aSpell Slots&r.

&6Cast your spells&r by holding your Spellbook in your main or offhand, and right click. &aSelect your spells with v&r.
