
# Wand



---
The &bCasting Wand&r is an item which you can assign a single spell to. It will automatically have the form &aProjectile&r and the Glyph of &aAccelerate&r.

&6Place it on your Scribe's Table&r and then create the desired spell in your Book. You can then &6shift right click&r on the Table with your Spellbook to write the spell to the wand.
