
# About Spawners

> A Different Kind Of Farming

---
&bSpawners&r can now be collected with Silk Touch! The &bSpawner Key&r will allow you to right click a &bSpawner&r and alter its functionality to &aincrease range or spawns&r, etc.

By default, any &bSpawners&r you pick up &awill be empty&r. You'll need to &6right click them with a spawn egg&r to assign them a mob. However, spawners collected via the &lCreate&r &bCart Assembler&r will keep their target.
