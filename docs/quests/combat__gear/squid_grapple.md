
# Squid Grapple



---
The &aGiant Squid&r is the natural prey of the &eCachelot Whale&r. When attacked by this predator, there is a chance it will drop one of its &bTentacles&r, which can be used to craft the &bGrappling Squok&r.

By firing the Squok like a bow, the player can &6grapple to high places&r or even &6suspend themselves in midair&r with up to 4 tentacles. &6Pressing shift&r will dismount the player from the Squok, and retract the tentacles.
