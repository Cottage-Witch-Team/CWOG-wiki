
# Making A Glyph

> Building Blocks Of Spells

---
For starters, let's make the &bHarvest Glyph&r. As you can see in &lJEI&r, it requires an &bIron Hoe&r and an &bEarth Essence&r.

To make the Earth Essence, you'll need to have your &aArcane Pedestals set up around your Imbuement Chamber&r. You will need &bDirt, Seeds, and an Iron Ingot&r. &6Right click each reagent on a pedestal&r (the order doesn't matter). Once they're in place, put a &bSource Gem&r into the Imbuement Chamber.

If you have done everything correctly, it will begin crafting! It may go slowly unless there is a &bfilled Source Jar&r within 6 blocks.

Once it's done, pull it from the Chamber and &6throw both items&r onto your Scribe's Table. Then, grab your finished &bGlyph&r and &6right click while holding it&r to unlock the knowledge!
