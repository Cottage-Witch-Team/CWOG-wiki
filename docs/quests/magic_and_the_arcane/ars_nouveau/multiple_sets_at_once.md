
# Multiple Sets At Once

There are so many options for **magely gear**{.aqua}... How can you choose? Well, you may not have to!

The **Wizard's Armarium**{.aqua} is a **Belt curio**{.green} which can contain up to **three sets of armor**{.green} and even **hotbar items**{.green} for you to swap on the fly.

By default, you'll **press 0**{.gold} to bring up a radial wheel of armor sets. But **first you need to load the Armarium**{.pink} with gear.

To begin, **equip your robes and set up your hotbar**{.gold}. Then, open the radial and **select the slot**{.gold} to save them to. Open your radial again, and **switch to a different slot**{.gold}. Equip your second set of armor and then **repeat the previous**{.gold} steps until all 3 slots are filled. Now, you can **easily swap between them**{.green} using the radial.
