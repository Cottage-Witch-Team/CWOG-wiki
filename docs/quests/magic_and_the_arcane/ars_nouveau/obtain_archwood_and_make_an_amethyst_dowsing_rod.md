
# Obtain Archwood And Make An Amethyst Dowsing Rod

> A Rare Tree And A Rare Gem

---
Your next step is to obtain &bArchwood&r of some type. These trees are found somewhat rarely in many biomes.

From the Archwood, you can craft the &bAmethyst Dowsing Rod&r, which will help you find &bAmethyst Geodes&r. This is an important magical material, so collect lots of it!
