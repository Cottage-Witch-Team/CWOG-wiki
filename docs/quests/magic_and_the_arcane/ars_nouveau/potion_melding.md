
# Potion Melding

> Combine Multiple Effects Into A Single Bottle!

---
The &bPotion Melder&r allows you to &amix the effects of two or more potions&r. It draws from &bPotion Jars&r which surround it on either side, and when &6provided with Source&r, outputs to a Jar beneath it.

Only two Potion Jars can be used at a time, however, you can &6fill one Jar with a Melded Potion&r to add even more effects to it.
