
# Potion Melding

> Combine multiple effects into a single bottle!

---


The **Potion Melder**{.aqua} allows you to **mix the effects of two or more potions**{.green}. It draws from **Potion Jars**{.aqua} which surround it on either side, and when **provided with Source**{.gold}, outputs to a Jar beneath it.

Only two Potion Jars can be used at a time, however, you can **fill one Jar with a Melded Potion**{.gold} to add even more effects to it.

---



!!! tip "TASK" 

	- Acquire **Potion Melder**
