
# Starbuncles Carry Liquid!

Equip your **Starbuncles**{.yellow} with a **Starbucket**{.aqua} to allow them to **transport fluid back and forth**{.green}. You may wish to store the fluid in a **Fluid Containment Jar**{.aqua}.
