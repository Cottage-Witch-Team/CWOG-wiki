
# Firenando Charm

Unlike the other **Charms**{.aqua}, the **Flarecannon**{.yellow}'s does not require **Shards**{.aqua}, and can be simply crafted.

The **Flarecannon**{.yellow} will **defend its territory**{.green} much like the **Weald Walkers**{.yellow}, by firing homing projectiles at enemies.

You can give them **Soul Sand**{.aqua} or **Magma Blocks**{.aqua} to change their appearance.
