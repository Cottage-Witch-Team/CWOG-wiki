
# Scroll Of Save Starbuncle

Although charming, **Starbuncles** are not the hardiest creatures, and it can be a pain to reinstruct them should something unpleasant happen to them.

Thankfully, the **Scroll of Save Starbuncle** will *preserve all their settings*. Just be sure to ==right click the scroll== on your Starby after you've finished setting them up!
