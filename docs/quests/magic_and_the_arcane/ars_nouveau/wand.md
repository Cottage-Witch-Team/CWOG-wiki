
# Wand

The **Casting Wand** is an item which you can assign a single spell to. It will automatically have the form *Projectile* and the Glyph of *Accelerate*.

==Place it on your Scribe's Table== and then create the desired spell in your Book. You can then ==shift right click== on the Table with your Spellbook to write the spell to the wand.
