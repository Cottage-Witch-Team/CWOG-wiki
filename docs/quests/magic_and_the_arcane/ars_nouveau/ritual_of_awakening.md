
# Ritual of Awakening

> An ent is never hasty

---



!!! tip "Task:"
	## Acquire :
	- Ritual Awakening
	- Dominion Wand

---


The **Weald Walkers** are Archtrees which have been awoken. In order to perform this Ritual, ==place the brazier near an Archwood tree== and ==right click== it with the Tablet. ==Right click== the Brazier to get started.

Once the **Weald Walker** is ambling about, ==right click it== with the **Dominion Wand**, and then right click it on the block you want as the Walker's *home*.
