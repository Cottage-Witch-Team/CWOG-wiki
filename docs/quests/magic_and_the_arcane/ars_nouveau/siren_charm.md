
# Siren Charm



---
&eSirens&r can be found rarely in &aOcean Biomes&r, and you can obtain their &bShards&r by giving them a &bSea Pickle&r, their favorite snack!

Use the &bSiren Charm&r on a piece of Prismarine to summon it. &eSirens&r will change color if given &bkelp&r or &bcorals&r.

The &bSiren Shrine&r will generate &afishing loot&r, as long as it is provided with &bSource&r. The &eSirens&r will produce faster if they have lots of aquatic plants and animals to keep them company!
