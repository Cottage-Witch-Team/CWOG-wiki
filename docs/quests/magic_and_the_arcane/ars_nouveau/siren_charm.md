
# Siren Charm

**Sirens**{.yellow} can be found rarely in **Ocean Biomes**{.green}, and you can obtain their **Shards**{.aqua} by giving them a **Sea Pickle**{.aqua}, their favorite snack!

Use the **Siren Charm**{.aqua} on a piece of Prismarine to summon it. **Sirens**{.yellow} will change color if given **kelp**{.aqua} or **corals**{.aqua}.

The **Siren Shrine**{.aqua} will generate **fishing loot**{.green}, as long as it is provided with **Source**{.aqua}. The **Sirens**{.yellow} will produce faster if they have lots of aquatic plants and animals to keep them company!
