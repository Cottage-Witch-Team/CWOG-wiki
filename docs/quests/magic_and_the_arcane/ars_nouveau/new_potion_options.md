
# New Potion Options

> The Potion Jar, Melder, And Flasks

---
The &bPotion Jar&r can hold up to 100 doses of a single potion.

You can use it to refill your &bPotion Flask&r, which is a container able to hold up to 8 doses of the same potion type.

There are two variants of the Potion Flask: &aExtend Time&r and &aAmplify&r. I think you can guess what each does.
