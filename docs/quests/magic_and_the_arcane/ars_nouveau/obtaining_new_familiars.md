
# Obtaining New Familiars

> Natural automation and friendly companions

---


**Familiars**{.yellow} are small creatures who help you with your magical duties. These differ from **Crows**{.yellow} in both how you meet them, and what they can do.

Firstly, you must obtain the **Charm**{.aqua} of each Familiar. This is done differently depending on which one you're trying to summon. But you will need to **activate their Charm**{.gold} before beginning the Ritual.

You must **place a Ritual Brazier**{.gold} nearby the Familiar and **put the **Tablet of Binding&6 on it**{.aqua}. Then &6right click**{.gold} the Brazier. It will ensnare the **Familiar**{.yellow} and produce a **Bound Script**{.aqua}, which you can then **right click**{.gold} to learn - from then on, that Familiar may be summoned from the **third tab in your spellbook**{.green}!

Read on for more information about each Familiar, both in Familiar mode and out.

You can dismiss your summoned Familiar with a **Dispel**{.green} Spell. Break the blocks they came from to **get back their Charm**{.green}.
