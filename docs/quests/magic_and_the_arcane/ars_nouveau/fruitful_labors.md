
# Fruitful Labors

Each type of **Archwood Tree**{.green} bears its own **fruit**{.aqua}! You can find it **growing from the tree like Cocoa Beans**{.green}.

The **Bastion Fruit**{.aqua} comes from the **Vexing Archwood**{.green} Tree. When consumed, it will grant you **Shielding**{.green}, a form of **Resistance**{.green}.

The **Frostaya**{.aqua} comes from the **Cascading Archwood**{.green} Tree. When consumed, it will **begin to freeze you**{.green}.

The **Mendosteen**{.aqua} comes from the **Flourishing Archwood**{.green} Tree. It grants the **Recovery**{.green} effect, which increases healing you receive from health items.

The **Bombegranate**{.aqua} comes from the **Blazing Archwood**{.green} Tree. **Its effects are unknown**{.pink}, as the journal from which I got this information ends at this entry.
