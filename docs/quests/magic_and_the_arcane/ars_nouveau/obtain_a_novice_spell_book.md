
# Obtain a Novice Spell Book

> Good beginner's luck!

---


To obtain the **Novice Spell Book**{.aqua}, you must **remove the Worn Notebook**{.gold} and craft it with **Gold**{.aqua} in a **Crafting Table**{.aqua}.

To retrieve your **Worn Notebook**{.aqua} from your **Akashic Tome**{.aqua}, **select the Worn Notebook in the tome**{.gold}, then **crouch and throw the book**{.gold}. This will eject the Worn Notebook so you can craft with it.

The name will look funny. That's normal, you can craft with it just fine.

Once you have your **Novice Spell Book**{.aqua}, you can **hold it and press c**{.gold} to access the interface.
The top tab is **the worn notebook, a reference guide for the mod**{.green}. The next tab lets you **build and save spells**{.green}. We'll address the rest later.

Note that **while holding your book, you cannot use the ` key**{.pink}.
