
# Obtain a Blood Sigil

Blood Sigils are used to collect blood for rituals. They are crafted with **Redstone** and **Polished Blackstone**.
Once you have crafted the Sigil, place it in the bottom of your **Mixing Cauldron** (by *right-clicking and placing it in the left slot of the crafting interface*).
Once it is installed, make sure there is no liquid in your Cauldron, and then *jump inside of it*. Blood must be taken from a willing participant, and that means you! Jump 3 times to obtain enough Blood for 1 Bottle. Then, right click the Cauldron with a **Glass Bottle** to collect it.
