
# Meet a Crow

> You look oddly familiar

---


Once you meet your first **Crow**{.yellow}, you're ready to tame. Bring lots of **Seed Mixture**{.aqua} because they can be hard to win over!

Once you have tamed the Crow Familiar, you can **crouch and right click it**{.gold} to **access its menu**{.green}.
If you **right click without crouching**{.gold}, you will **change its mode**{.green} to Sit, Follow, and more. You can do that in the menu, as well. By default, it will Follow and sit on your shoulder, unless you crouch.

**Crows can be equipped with hats and weapons**{.green} via the menu. It can wear most helmets and wield most melee weapons. Unfortunately, **they cannot wear hats from the **Simple Hats**{.glow}&d mod**{.pink}. :(
{image:hexerei:textures/book/pictures/crow_gui.png width:100 height:150 align:1}
Through the menu, you can instruct your **Familiar**{.yellow} to help you farm your crops, or to follow you in combat, or even to **pickpocket villagers**{.green}.
