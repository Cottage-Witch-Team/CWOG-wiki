
# Crow Flute

> Utterly charming

---

# Task:

- Acquire Crow Flute
---

Crows love music! If you ever lose track of them, you can instantly summon them using the **Crow Flute**. You'll need **Mahogany Wood** and some **Dye** in order to craft.

==Right click while holding the Flute== to play a tune to call your **Familiars**.

If you ==crouch and right click a Crow with the Flute==, you can Select it with the *Select* button. Then, you can select *Perch*, and ==crouch and right click a block/inventory item with the Flute== in order to tell your Crow to perch there. Crows will bring items back to their perch when told to *Gather*.
