
# Obtain a Dipper

> Is a "big dipper" joke too obvious?

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Candle Dipper
---


The **Dipper** is used for making candles. It can be placed above a **Mixing Cauldron&b filled with &bTallow** (rendered Animal Fat) and hung with String in order to ==create Candles==.

Unlike basic Candles, these can be placed together with Candles of different colors, and may be lit separately!

**Tallow** is created in the Mixing Cauldron with **Animal Fat and Honeycombs**.
