
# Craft a Dowsing Rod

> A magical compass to lead the way

---


In order to locate much of the content of this mod, one must first locate a *Swamp* or *Jungle* biome. These can be found either with the **Nature's Compass**, or the **Dowsing Rod**.

The **Dowsing rod**'s leaves will *change colors when it points towards* one of the aforementioned biomes. If one crafts it into the **Nature's Compass**, you can *search the surrounding area* for any biome.
