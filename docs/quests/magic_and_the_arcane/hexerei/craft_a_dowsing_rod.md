
# Craft a Dowsing Rod

> A magical compass to lead the way

---


In order to locate much of the content of this mod, one must first locate a **Swamp**{.green} or **Jungle**{.green} biome. These can be found either with the **Nature's Compass**{.aqua}, or the **Dowsing Rod**{.aqua}.

The **Dowsing rod**{.aqua}'s leaves will **change colors when it points towards**{.green} one of the aforementioned biomes. If one crafts it into the **Nature's Compass**{.aqua}, you can **search the surrounding area**{.green} for any biome.
