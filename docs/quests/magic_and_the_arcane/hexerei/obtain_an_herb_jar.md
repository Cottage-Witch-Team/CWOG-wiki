
# Obtain an Herb Jar

> Totally herbaceous, dude

---


The **Herb Jar**{.aqua} can be crafted by smelting 8 **Sand**{.aqua} in your **Mixing Cauldron**{.aqua}. Each Herb Jar can carry **only one type**{.green} of Herb, but can **contain up to 1024**{.green} of that herb!

The Herb Jar label will **reflect what is stored inside of it**{.green}, and can be named using an **Anvil**{.aqua}. When broken, the Herb Jar retains its inventory.

**Left click an **Herb Jar to withdraw**{.aqua} 1 item. &6Crouch and left click**{.gold} to remove a stack.

**Familiar Crows**{.yellow} will **gather and deposit harvested herbs**{.green} if the button inside the jar (**crouch right click**{.gold} to access) is pressed.
