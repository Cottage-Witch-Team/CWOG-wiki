
# Book Of Shadows

> A witch's best friend

---


One of the most important tools in a witch's bag is their **Book of Shadows**, in which they record their knowledge and experience as they follow their path.

This one is helpfully *pre-filled with lots of information* about the magic in the world around you, although it's not a complete guide to everything.

In order to read your **Book of Shadows**, you must first place it on an **Altar**.
