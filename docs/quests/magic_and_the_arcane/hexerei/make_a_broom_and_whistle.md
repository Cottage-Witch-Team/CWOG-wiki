
# Make A Broom and Whistle

> Any kind works!

---

# Task:
## Acquire :
- Broom Whistle
- Any Brooms

---

There are three types of **Brooms**, *Willow*, *Witch Hazel*, and *Mahogany*. The Witch Hazel and Willow brooms are affordable to make, but slower to control and flammable. The Mahogany broom is faster and fire resistant- a worthy upgrade!

You can ==crouch and right click== your **Broom** in order to access its customization options, as well as switch it from *Float Mode* to *Rest Mode*, which determines what the Broom will do when you dismount.

You will also need a **Broom Whistle**.

Lost your broom? No worries! Just ==right click== with your **Broom Whistle** to call it to you!
First, you'll need to ==place the **Whistle&6 and &bBroom** in a crafting grid== to bind them. You can *unbind* them by &6putting the **Whistle** in alone.

Be aware that this ^^works best when your broom is in sight^^. It will try to fly to you, but it's just a broom so it's *not very smart* and often gets stuck on things.
