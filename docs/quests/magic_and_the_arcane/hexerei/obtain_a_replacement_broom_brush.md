
# Obtain A Replacement Broom Brush

> Re-up Your Flight Subscription

---
As you use your Broom, the Brush will eventually lose durability and need replacing. Begin by making the standard &bWet Broom Brush&r, which can be either immediately upgraded to an &bEnhanced Broom Brush&r, or dried out for use on a &bDrying Rack&r.
