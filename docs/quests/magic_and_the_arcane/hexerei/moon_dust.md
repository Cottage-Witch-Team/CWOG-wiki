
# Moon Dust

> Redstone

---
Similar to moon water in real life, you can obtain &bMoon Dust&r by combining &bRedstone&r and &bGlowstone&r &6during a Full Moon&r.

It's used for some important recipes, such high level &bStorage Glyphs&r, so it's a good idea to have some on hand! You can use a &bMoon Dial&r to tell what moon phase is currently active. You can &6hold it and right click&r to see how many days remain until the full moon.
