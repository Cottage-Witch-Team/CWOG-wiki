
# Moon Dust

Similar to moon water in real life, you can obtain **Moon Dust** by combining **Redstone** and **Glowstone** ==during a Full Moon==.

It's used for some important recipes, such high level **Storage Glyphs**, so it's a good idea to have some on hand! You can use a **Moon Dial** to tell what moon phase is currently active. You can ==hold it and right click== to see how many days remain until the full moon.

---



!!! tip ""
	### Acquire :
	- **Redstone (4)**
	- **Glowstone Dust (4)**
