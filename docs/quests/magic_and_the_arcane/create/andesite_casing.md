
# Andesite Casing



---
&bAndesite Casing&r is one of three different tiers of Casing, used to craft various machinery. It is crafted by applying &bAndesite Alloy&r to a &bStripped Log&r. You can do this &6manually by placing the &bStripped Log&r in world and &6right clicking it with the &bAndesite Alloy&r. It can also be automated by using a &bDeployer&r.
