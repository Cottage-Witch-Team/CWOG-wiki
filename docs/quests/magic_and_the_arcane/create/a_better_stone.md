
# A Better Stone


!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Andesite Alloy (4)
---


**Andesite** is one of the foundational blocks of beginning your factory. However, though stone by itself is strong, it is not malleable. So, it is best to mix it with **Iron** to create a more versatile material: **Andesite Alloy**.
**Andesite Alloy** is used in making both **Shafts** (which are then used to make **Cogs**), and also to reinforce stripped logs into **Andesite Casing**. Together, these make up the basis of most of the recipes in this mod.
==Equip these Goggles in your Curio slot== to *easily see detailed information* about all your machines.
