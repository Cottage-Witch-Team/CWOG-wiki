
# A Better Stone

**Andesite**{.aqua} is one of the foundational blocks of beginning your factory. However, though stone by itself is strong, it is not malleable. So, it is best to mix it with **Iron**{.aqua} to create a more versatile material: **Andesite Alloy**{.aqua}.
**Andesite Alloy**{.aqua} is used in making both **Shafts**{.aqua} (which are then used to make **Cogs**{.aqua}), and also to reinforce stripped logs into **Andesite Casing**{.aqua}. Together, these make up the basis of most of the recipes in this mod.
**Equip these Goggles in your Curio slot**{.gold} to **easily see detailed information**{.green} about all your machines.
