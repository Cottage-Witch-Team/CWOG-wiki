
# Moving and Storing Fluids

For certain recipes you may wish to use **Fluids**, and for that you'll need **Tanks**, **Pipes**, and **Pumps**.
To test it out, why not ^^set up an infinite **Lava&d source**? Set up a basic &aLava Generator^^ with **Dripstone** and a **Cauldron**.
Then, place a **Pump** and/or some **Fluid Pipes** beside it, as well as a **Tank** to hold the liquid. Power the pump and ==use the Wrench to change the Pump's direction==.

---



!!! tip ""
	### Acquire :
	- **Fluid Tank**
	- **Fluid Pipe**
	- **Mechanical Pump**
