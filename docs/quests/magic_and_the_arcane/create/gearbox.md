
# Gearbox

The **Gearbox**{.aqua} helps you **manipulate rotation**{.green} in a small space by **forming a right angle**{.green} for 2 or more **Cogs**{.aqua} or **Shafts**{.aqua}.
The **Gear Shift**{.aqua} can be **rotated on either x or y axis**{.gold} to help you **turn corners**{.pink} and **change directions**{.green}.
