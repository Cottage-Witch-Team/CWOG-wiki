
# Dispensing Fluids

Now that you have a **Pump and Pipes**, what do you do with the fluid?

Well, you have a few options. You could *compact the fluid* into solids using a **Mechanical Press** (see the other quests for this!), or you could use a **Spout** to *dispense fluid onto or into items* on a Depot.

Spouts can be used in making **Chocolate-Covered Berries**, **Honeyed Apples**, **Sweet Rolls**, and eventually also **Blaze Cakes**!
