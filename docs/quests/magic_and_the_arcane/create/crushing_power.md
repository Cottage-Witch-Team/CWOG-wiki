
# Crushing Power

One of the recipes that you can craft with your **Mechanical Crafters**{.aqua} is the **Crushing Wheel**{.aqua}. A pair of these wheels will **process raw ores and many other items**{.green}, giving you access to **bonus items**{.green} when compared with preparing them manually.

You'll also need them in your quest to &amake **Blaze Cakes**{.aqua}. Take this **Netherrack**{.aqua} and put it through the **Crushers**{.aqua}!
