
# Crushing Power

One of the recipes that you can craft with your **Mechanical Crafters** is the **Crushing Wheel**. A pair of these wheels will *process raw ores and many other items*, giving you access to *bonus items* when compared with preparing them manually.

You'll also need them in your quest to &amake **Blaze Cakes**. Take this **Netherrack** and put it through the **Crushers**!
