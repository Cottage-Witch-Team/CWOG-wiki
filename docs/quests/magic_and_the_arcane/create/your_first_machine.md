
# Your First Machine


!!! tip "Task:"
	## Acquire :
	- Mechanical Press
	- Hand Crank

---


One of the first machines you'll want to build is the **Mechanical Press**. This, well, presses items down to create things like **Brass and Iron Sheets**, which you'll need for future projects.
To begin with, make a **Hand Crank** to power it. Going forward, you'll *automate your power*, but in a pinch, some elbow grease does the trick just as well.
Take this **Iron** and place it underneath the **Mechanical Press**.
