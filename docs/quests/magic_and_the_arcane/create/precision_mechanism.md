
# Precision Mechanism

The **Precision Mechanism** is crafted by setting up a **Mechanical Belt** and **three Deployers**.
You can view the recipe in ***JEI*** by selecting the second tab at the top, ^^"Recipe Sequence"^^.
{image:cottagewitch:textures/precisionmechanism.png width:150 height:150 align:1}
The first Deployer should hold a **Cogwheel**, the second a **Large Cogwheel**, and the third a **Iron Nugget**. Each mechanism must ==go through the process 5 times==. There is only an ^^80% change of receiving^^ the final product.
