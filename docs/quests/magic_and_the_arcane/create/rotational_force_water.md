
# Rotational Force: Water

An early game method of generating rotational force is with water wheels. They require **at least one flowing water block**{.green} to function. Any additional flowing water blocks **will not generate more stress units or rotational speed**{.green}. The two types of water wheels are the standard **Water Wheel**{.aqua} and the **Large Water Wheel**{.aqua}. The standard **Water Wheel**{.aqua} generates **256 su**{.green} at a speed of **8 rpm**{.green}, while the **Large Water Wheel**{.aqua} generates **512 su**{.green} at a speed of **4 rpm**{.green}.


{image:cottagewitch:textures/lgwaterwheel.png width:100 height:100 align:1}

You can connect items such as **Shafts**{.aqua}, **Cogs**{.aqua}, or **Gearboxes**{.aqua} to the center of the wheel, allowing you to distribute rotational force to your machines.

You can also **right-click**{.gold} the water wheel with a **wooden plank**{.aqua} to change the texture of the water wheel to the corresponding plank texture. Keep in mind that modded planks **may cause odd textures**{.pink}.
