
# Rotation Speed Controller

The **Rotational Speed Controller**{.aqua} is a machine which can **increase, decrease, or reverse**{.green} the speed and direction of rotation.
**Place it down and connect one of the sides**{.gold} to your su network. Then, **attach a **Large Cogwheel**{.aqua} as shown in the image, and &6connect the subsequent machinery**{.gold} to that **Cogwheel**{.aqua}.
The machinery will then adopt whatever speed you **set the Controller to**{.green}. **Scroll to the negatives to reverse direction**{.gold}.
