
# Rotation Speed Controller


!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Rotation Speed Controller
---


The **Rotational Speed Controller** is a machine which can *increase, decrease, or reverse* the speed and direction of rotation.
==Place it down and connect one of the sides== to your su network. Then, ==attach a **Large Cogwheel** as shown in the image, and &6connect the subsequent machinery== to that **Cogwheel**.
The machinery will then adopt whatever speed you *set the Controller to*. ==Scroll to the negatives to reverse direction==.
