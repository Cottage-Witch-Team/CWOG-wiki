
# The Power of Rotational Force

*Rotational force* is measured in ^^Stress Units, or su^^. The more force you generate, the more su you have access to. Machines require a certain amount of su to run, and if you try to place too many operations on a single network, your system can become ^^Overstressed^^. You can view this in individual parts by ==wearing Goggles==, but it is also recommended to craft a **Stressometer** to attach to your network.
The Stressometer allows you to *view your maximum su and how much of it is remaining*.
