
# The Power of Rotational Force

**Rotational force**{.green} is measured in **Stress Units, or su**{.pink}. The more force you generate, the more su you have access to. Machines require a certain amount of su to run, and if you try to place too many operations on a single network, your system can become **Overstressed**{.pink}. You can view this in individual parts by **wearing Goggles**{.gold}, but it is also recommended to craft a **Stressometer**{.aqua} to attach to your network.
The Stressometer allows you to **view your maximum su and how much of it is remaining**{.green}.
