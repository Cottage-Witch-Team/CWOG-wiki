
# Soul Prison



---
The journals from which I discovered these dungeons had this to say: "Found in the lava pools of the Nether’s &csoul sand valley&r, you may find this &cprison of souls&r. With the amount of &eghasts&r surrounding it, approaching and reaching the top for your reward may prove to be no small task. Be wary as the ghasts are not opposed to &adestroying the structure around you&r with their fireballs. Should you survive, you may walk out with a &bSoul Cannon&r."
