
# Claiming Your Space \& Chunk Loading

> When you're away, your base will play

---


If you want your base to stay active even when you're not there, you want **chunk loading**{.green}. This will also help if you **have a vanilla-style mob farm**{.green}.

Open your inventory and look at the top left of your screen. The **map icon**{.green} will lead you to the **FTB Chunks**{.green} screen.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/ftbmenu.png width:200 height:150 align:1}

Choose **Claimed Chunks**{.aqua} and **left click**{.gold} on a chunk to claim it for your own. To unclaim, **right click**{.aqua} it. To **load the chunk when you're not in it**{.green}, **shift and left click the claimed chunk**{.gold}. You can unload it by holding **shift and right clicking**{.gold}.

{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/claimchunks.png width:200 height:120 align:1}

When a chunk is claimed, **other server members cannot break or interact with blocks in it**{.pink}. In order to grant someone access to your chunks, you need to **add them or their Team as an Ally**{.green}.
