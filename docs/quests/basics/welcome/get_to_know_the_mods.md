
# Get to know the mods

> Take a little look-see

---


This modpack has a number of building and decoration mods which are all worth looking into. **Most of them do not have associated quests**{.pink}.

So how are you supposed to know they're there? Why, **JEI**{.glow} of course. **Opening your inventory**{.gold} will show you a wall of items to your right. You can search through these items at the bottom right. Type **@**{.pink} and the name of a mod to search for only items from that mod.

For example, you can **search "@another"**{.green} to view everything from Another Furniture. **Left click the item**{.gold} to view its crafting recipe. **Right click it**{.gold} to view what recipes it's used in.

This will let you view how to make anything in the game!
