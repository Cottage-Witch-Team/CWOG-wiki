
# Extreme Sound Muffler

> Selective hearing. literally.

# Task:

- Extreme Sound Muffler
---

This modpack adds a lot of new sounds. So to help you filter the ones you don't like, it also includes *Super Sound Muffler*.

==Press F6 to open the menu==. From here, you can click *Recent* to see what you've recently heard. You can play the sound to confirm it's the one you don't like, and then ==mute it!==
