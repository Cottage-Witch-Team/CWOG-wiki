
# The Only Way to "Fly"

> Slimy transport

---


Need to get somewhere in a hurry? Why not try **slinging**{.green}? The **Slime Sling**{.aqua}, when **pointed at the ground and drawn**{.gold}, will shoot the player in the opposite direction upon release.

You can achieve **quite a bit of air time**{.green} this way! And the **Slime Boots**{.aqua} make it so you...you know...**don't die from fall damage**{.green}. A winning combination!
