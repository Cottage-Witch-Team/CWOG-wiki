# Task:

- The Only Way to "Fly"
---

Need to get somewhere in a hurry? Why not try *slinging*? The **Slime Sling**, when ==pointed at the ground and drawn==, will shoot the player in the opposite direction upon release.

You can achieve *quite a bit of air time* this way! And the **Slime Boots** make it so you...you know...*don't die from fall damage*. A winning combination!
