
# Begin your World

> Welcome in!

---


Congratulations on entering the Overworld for the first time! It's good to have you here. You can **use these quests as a sort of guide**{.gold} to help you through mods you might not be familiar with!

You now have a **backpack**{.aqua} to help ease your loot goblin woes - take **very good care of it**{.green} because &dthe only place to obtain **Backpacks**{.aqua} is from **End City**{.red} chests!

This quest also grants you an **Akashic Tome**{.aqua}, which contains guidebooks to mods in the pack. **If you ever lose your Akashic Tome**{.blue}, you can easily **craft a new one**{.gold} with a **Book**{.aqua} and a **Stick**{.aqua}.

Your most useful tool is **JEI**{.blue}, a mod that appears on the right of your inventory screen. It will let you view all the items in the modpack, and their recipes. **Please explore this to see what is on offer**{.gold}!
