
# Begin your World

> Welcome in!

---


Congratulations on entering the Overworld for the first time! It's good to have you here. You can ==use these quests as a sort of guide== to help you through mods you might not be familiar with!

You now have a **backpack** to help ease your loot goblin woes - take *very good care of it* because &dthe only place to obtain **Backpacks** is from *End City* chests!

This quest also grants you an **Akashic Tome**, which contains guidebooks to mods in the pack. ***If you ever lose your Akashic Tome***, you can easily ==craft a new one== with a **Book** and a **Stick**.

Your most useful tool is ***JEI***, a mod that appears on the right of your inventory screen. It will let you view all the items in the modpack, and their recipes. ==Please explore this to see what is on offer==!
