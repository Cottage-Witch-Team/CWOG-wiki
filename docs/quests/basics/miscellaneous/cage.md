
# Cage

> Despite all my rage, i am still just a...oh you know

---


The **Cage** is similar to the **Capture Cell**, in that you can ==right click it on a small mob== to pick it up and transport it. You can ==right click a block to release== the mob, or you can ==shift right click== to place the **Cage** down and display your new friend in their enclosure!
