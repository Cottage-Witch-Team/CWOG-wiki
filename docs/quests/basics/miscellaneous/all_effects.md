
# All Effects

Those who experiment with altering their states may find themselves in quite the situation, not entirely knowing how they arrived at this conclusion.

This achievement involves getting *every vanilla effect* at once, as well as the following modded effects:

Sculk Affinity (Deeper and Darker), Panic, Serenity (both Environmental), Health Boost, Resilience (Quark).
