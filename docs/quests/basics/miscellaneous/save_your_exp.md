
# Save your EXP

> Unfortunately not the experience you can put on your resume

---


A piece of **Carved Nephrite** allows you to store your Experience in a network, to prevent it being lost or reduced on death. Connect multiple **Carved Nephrite** together to store even more.

It also allows you to *share experience with others*!

Additionally, you can store up to 30 levels in a **Nephrite Ring**.

Stand on the **Siphon** to drain your EXP, and activate the **Fountain** to shower yourself in it.

---



!!! tip 'TASK' 
	### Acquire :
	- **Carved Nephrite**
	- **Nephrite Siphon**
	- **Nephrite Spout**
