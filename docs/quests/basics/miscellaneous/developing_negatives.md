
# Developing Negatives

> A positive development

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Developing Negatives
---


In order to *print your photographs*, you must first *develop the film*.

This is done by crafting **Thick**, **Awkward**, and **Mundane Potions** together with the **Color Film**. For **Black and White Film**, you only need a **Bottle of Water**.

Simply ==combine them== in a **Crafting Grid**.
