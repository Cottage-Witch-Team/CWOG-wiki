
# Obtain a Glow Stick

> Peace, love, unity, glowstick

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Any Glowsticks
---


**Glow Sticks** are a light source that can be *placed on any surface* or *thrown like a snowball* (placing itself wherever it lands).

They can be *thrown and placed underwater*, making them ideal for subnautical exploration.

Glow sticks also come in all 16 colors as well as the glow ink hue, making them ideal for *marking out pathways* to different locations.
