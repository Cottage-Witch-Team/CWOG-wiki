
# Obtain a Glow Stick

> Peace, love, unity, glowstick

---


**Glow Sticks**{.aqua} are a light source that can be **placed on any surface**{.green} or **thrown like a snowball**{.green} (placing itself wherever it lands).

They can be **thrown and placed underwater**{.green}, making them ideal for subnautical exploration.

Glow sticks also come in all 16 colors as well as the glow ink hue, making them ideal for **marking out pathways**{.green} to different locations.
