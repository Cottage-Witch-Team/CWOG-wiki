
# Portal Fluid Bottle

**Dimensional Tears**{.aqua} are a fluid which **teleports an entity**{.green} to its point of origin. If a **player**{.yellow} has a **respawn point**{.green} set, it will bring them there. If they don't, and for **all other entities**{.yellow}, it will transport them to **world spawn**{.red}.

You can obtain this item from **certain chests**{.green}, or by **right clicking a bottle**{.gold} onto **Crying Obsidian**{.aqua} or an activated **Respawn Anchor**{.aqua}.

To use the item, either **drink it from the bottle**{.gold} or **place it in a cauldron**{.gold} and jump into it.
