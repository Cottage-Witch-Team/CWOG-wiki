
# Portal Fluid Bottle

**Dimensional Tears** are a fluid which *teleports an entity* to its point of origin. If a **player** has a *respawn point* set, it will bring them there. If they don't, and for **all other entities**, it will transport them to *world spawn*.

You can obtain this item from *certain chests*, or by ==right clicking a bottle== onto **Crying Obsidian** or an activated **Respawn Anchor**.

To use the item, either ==drink it from the bottle== or ==place it in a cauldron== and jump into it.
