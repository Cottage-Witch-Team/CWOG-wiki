
# Snail Mail

> A way to communicate in multiplayer

---


You can send letters and packages to your friends! You can *send and receive mail* using a **Snailbox**. Placing it down claims it, and you can even give it a name by ==right clicking on it== and entering text in the name field.
To *send mail*, you'll need a **Stamp** and an **Open Envelope**. These envelopes can be reused, so they're good for the environment. ==Place the Envelope in the upper right of the **Snailbox**. Then click &6"Open Envelope==."
{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/snailmail1.png width:250 height:300 align:1}
In here, you can put your mail! You can send **postcards, wrapped parcels, and more**! *Don't forget to add a Stamp*! Enter the name of the recipient, your name if desired, and then ==Hit Esc to go back to the previous window==!
{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/snailmail2.png width:250 height:200 align:1}
Once you've hit ^^Esc^^, you can *select the Snailbox to send to*, and ta-da! You're done!
{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/snailmail3.png width:250 height:80 align:1}
{image:cottagewitch:textures/quest_images/snailmail4.png width:250 height:150 align:1}
