
# Sea Witch

Although not all mermaids are witches, there are some *notable examples of some which are*. You, too, can keep a creepy garden under the sea with the help of the **Sea Necklace**, which will equip you with a *tail all your own!* It will also *grant you swim speed bonuses* and the *ability to breathe* underwater indefinitely.

To craft it, you'll need a **Sea Crystal**, which you can craft with a little luck at treasure-hunting!
