
# Sea Witch

Although not all mermaids are witches, there are some **notable examples of some which are**{.green}. You, too, can keep a creepy garden under the sea with the help of the **Sea Necklace**{.aqua}, which will equip you with a **tail all your own!**{.green} It will also **grant you swim speed bonuses**{.green} and the **ability to breathe**{.green} underwater indefinitely.

To craft it, you'll need a **Sea Crystal**{.aqua}, which you can craft with a little luck at treasure-hunting!
