
# Obtain Magnetite

> Magnetite Chunk

---
&bMagnetite&r generates in small clusters deep underground, and are surrounded by &bLava&r and &bDeepslate Iron&r, encased in a shell of &bObsidian&r. These geodes are somewhat rare to come across.

You'll need &aSilk Touch&r or a &bHammer and Chisel&r to obtain the blocks, otherwise you will get &bMagnetite Chunks&r instead, which can be used in crafting.
