
# Obtain Magnetite

**Magnetite**{.aqua} generates in small clusters deep underground, and are surrounded by **Lava**{.aqua} and **Deepslate Iron**{.aqua}, encased in a shell of **Obsidian**{.aqua}. These geodes are somewhat rare to come across.

You'll need **Silk Touch**{.green} or a **Hammer and Chisel**{.aqua} to obtain the blocks, otherwise you will get **Magnetite Chunks**{.aqua} instead, which can be used in crafting.
