
# Take Your Biomes With You

Have you ever fallen in love with the color palette of one biome, but your base is somewhere else entirely? Well, worry not! With **Biome Jars**{.aqua}, you can **pick up the biome and transport it!**{.gold}
Right click the **Biome Jar**{.aqua} on the ground of the biome you want to take. Wait just long enough to go - "Is this worki -"

****BANG!**{.blue} Yes, it's working! Once it completes its capture, you can &6right click it**{.pink} to pick it back up.
Then, take your jar and **right click it**{.gold} on the area you want to transfer the new biome to.

	- Acquire **Empty Biome Jar**
