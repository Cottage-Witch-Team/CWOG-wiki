
# Take Your Biomes With You

Have you ever fallen in love with the color palette of one biome, but your base is somewhere else entirely? Well, worry not! With **Biome Jars**, you can ==pick up the biome and transport it!==
Right click the **Biome Jar** on the ground of the biome you want to take. Wait just long enough to go - "Is this worki -"

***^^BANG!*** Yes, it's working! Once it completes its capture, you can &6right click it^^ to pick it back up.
Then, take your jar and ==right click it== on the area you want to transfer the new biome to.
