
# Squid Grapple

The *Giant Squid* is the natural prey of the **Cachelot Whale**. When attacked by this predator, there is a chance it will drop one of its **Tentacles**, which can be used to craft the **Grappling Squok**.

By firing the Squok like a bow, the player can ==grapple to high places== or even ==suspend themselves in midair== with up to 4 tentacles. ==Pressing shift== will dismount the player from the Squok, and retract the tentacles.
