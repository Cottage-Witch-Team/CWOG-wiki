
# Squid Grapple

The **Giant Squid**{.green} is the natural prey of the **Cachelot Whale**{.yellow}. When attacked by this predator, there is a chance it will drop one of its **Tentacles**{.aqua}, which can be used to craft the **Grappling Squok**{.aqua}.

By firing the Squok like a bow, the player can **grapple to high places**{.gold} or even **suspend themselves in midair**{.gold} with up to 4 tentacles. **Pressing shift**{.gold} will dismount the player from the Squok, and retract the tentacles.
