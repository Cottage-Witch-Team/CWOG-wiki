
# Customize Your Elytra

Are you tired of the basic Luna Moth **Elytra**{.aqua} and want to add some more **pizazz**{.green}? **Place it in a crafting grid**{.gold} and you'll get two separate wings from it.

Each **Wing**{.aqua} can be crafted with **Dye**{.aqua} or **Banners**{.aqua} to take on their appearance.

Be aware that **Modded banner patterns do not work**{.pink}!

!!! tip "TASK" 

	- Acquire **Elytra Wing**
