
# Customize Your Elytra

Are you tired of the basic Luna Moth **Elytra** and want to add some more *pizazz*? ==Place it in a crafting grid== and you'll get two separate wings from it.

Each **Wing** can be crafted with **Dye** or **Banners** to take on their appearance.

Be aware that ^^Modded banner patterns do not work^^!
