
# Gild Your Netherite Gear

> Gild your loins! or head, or chest, or...

---


It's frustrating to have to switch from your **Netherite Armor**{.aqua} to Gold simply to appease the **Piglins**{.yellow}. Well, no more!

Now you can **gild your Netherite armor**{.green} in a **Smithing Station**{.aqua} to enhance it with enough gold to pacify the **Piglins**{.yellow}.

---



!!! tip "TASK" 

	- Acquire **Any Gilded Netherite Items**
