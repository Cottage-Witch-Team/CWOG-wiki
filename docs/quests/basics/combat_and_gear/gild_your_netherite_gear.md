
# Gild Your Netherite Gear

> Gild your loins! or head, or chest, or...

---


It's frustrating to have to switch from your **Netherite Armor** to Gold simply to appease the **Piglins**. Well, no more!

Now you can *gild your Netherite armor* in a **Smithing Station** to enhance it with enough gold to pacify the **Piglins**.

---



!!! tip 'TASK' 

	- Acquire **Any Gilded Netherite Items**
---
