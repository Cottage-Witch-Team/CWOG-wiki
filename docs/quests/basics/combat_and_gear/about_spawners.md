
# About Spawners

> A different kind of farming

---



!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Spawner Key
---


**Spawners** can now be collected with Silk Touch! The **Spawner Key** will allow you to right click a **Spawner** and alter its functionality to *increase range or spawns*, etc.

By default, any **Spawners** you pick up *will be empty*. You'll need to ==right click them with a spawn egg== to assign them a mob. However, spawners collected via the ***Create*** **Cart Assembler** will keep their target.
