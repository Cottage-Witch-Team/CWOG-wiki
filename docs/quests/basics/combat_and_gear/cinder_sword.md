
# Cinder Sword

> The downside is killing cindershells

---


Made from fallen **Cindershells**{.yellow}, a passive mob in the Nether, this sword has a strong affinity for **Lava**{.aqua}!

**Right click the blade on Lava**{.gold} to recharge it, providing it with a damage boost and the Flame effect!

---



!!! tip "TASK" 

	- Acquire **Cinder Sword**
