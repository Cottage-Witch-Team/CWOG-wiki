
# Tarot Trading

> Another chance at justice

---


Hold 2 Tarot Cards in your inventory and turn in this quest to receive a random Tarot card in exchange. You may get lucky!

This is for players who have collected many duplicates and want a shot at a different type. ^^This quest is repeatable^^.

!!! tip "Task:"

	- Acquire Any Tarot Cards (2)
