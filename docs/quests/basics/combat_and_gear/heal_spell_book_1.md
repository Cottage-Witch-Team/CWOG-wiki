
# Heal Spell Book 1

> Why do cactus need totems of undying?

---


This **Healing Tome** was created by a shaman of the **Cactem**, a desert-dwelling plantlike species which have a love for **Totems of Undying**.

Trade them Totems for a chance at obtaining this reusable spell book. It's a rare chance, but if you have the Totems to spare...

Best of all, if you obtain enough Tomes, you can ==combine them for a more powerful heal==! The books can go up to level 3.
