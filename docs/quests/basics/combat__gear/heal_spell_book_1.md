
# Heal Spell Book 1

> Why Do Cactus Need Totems Of Undying?

---
This &bHealing Tome&r was created by a shaman of the &eCactem&r, a desert-dwelling plantlike species which have a love for &bTotems of Undying&r.

Trade them Totems for a chance at obtaining this reusable spell book. It's a rare chance, but if you have the Totems to spare...

Best of all, if you obtain enough Tomes, you can &6combine them for a more powerful heal&r! The books can go up to level 3.
