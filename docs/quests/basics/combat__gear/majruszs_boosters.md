
# Majrusz's Boosters

> Boosty Collins

---
&bBoosters&r allow you to add additional bonuses to your &1Majrusz's Accessories&r! Standard accessories will only allow you to attach a single booster- but &bRunes&r and the &bSoul Of Minecraft&r will allow to you to attach 2 or even 3! The effects of multiple boosters can stack.

To attach a booster, simply &6combine the accessory and booster together in a crafting table&r. To remove the booster, you will need a &bRemoval Card&r.
