# Ritual of Awakening

> An ent is never hasty

The **Weald Walkers**{.yellow} are Archtrees which have been awoken. In order to perform this Ritual, **place the brazier near an Archwood tree**{.gold} and **right click**{.gold} it with the Tablet. **Right click**{.gold} the Brazier to get started.



Once the **Weald Walker**{.yellow} is ambling about, **right click it**{.gold} with the **Dominion Wand**{.aqua}, and then right click it on the block you want as the Walker's **home**{.green}.
