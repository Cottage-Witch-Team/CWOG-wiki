# Wand

The **Casting Wand**{.aqua} is an item which you can assign a single spell to. It will automatically have the form **Projectile**{.green} and the Glyph of **Accelerate**{.green}. **Place it on your Scribe's Table**{.gold} and then create the desired spell in your Book. You can then **shift right click**{.gold} on the Table with your Spellbook to write the spell to the wand.
