# Wixie Charm

> The world's cutest crafting table

The **Wixie**{.aqua} 's **Shards**{.aqua} are obtained by using the **Dispel**{.green} effect on a **Witch**{.green} when they're under half health. Once you craft the **Charm**{.aqua}, you can **right click it on a**{.gold} **Cauldron**{.aqua} to summon the **Wixie**{.green}.



Wixies will **increase the duration of consumed potions**{.green} while they are following you. They will also **apply negative potion effects on enemies**{.green}.



When a Wixie is bound to a Cauldron, it will **automatically craft an item or potion**{.green} it is told to. It will draw from nearby inventories, and can be paused if it receives a redstone signal.
