# Casting Crystal

A **Casting Crystal**{.aqua} is an item which can have a **spell bound to it**{.green}, and then you can **equip it as a Curio**{.gold}, and access its spell via a hotkey.



You can **wear up to three**{.green} of these, and by default they are accessible with **CTRL+1-3**{.gold}.
