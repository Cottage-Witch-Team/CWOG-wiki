# Amethyst Golem Charm

The **Amethyst Golem**{.aqua} can be summoned by using the **Ritual of Awakening**{.gold} on some **Budding Amethyst**{.aqua}. **Right click**{.gold} the shard on a block to summon the **Golem**{.yellow}, and then **set its home block**{.gold} with your **Dominion Wand**{.aqua}. **Amethyst Golems**{.yellow} will convert Amethyst into **Budding Amethyst**{.aqua}, speed up **amethyst growth**{.green}, and will also **harvest amethyst clusters**{.green}, so long as their home block is an inventory such as a chest or barrel.
