# Scroll Of Save Starbuncle

Although charming, **Starbuncles**{.yellow} are not the hardiest creatures, and it can be a pain to reinstruct them should something unpleasant happen to them.



Thankfully, the **Scroll of Save Starbuncle**{.aqua} will **preserve all their settings**{.green}. Just be sure to **right click the scroll**{.gold} on your Starby after you've finished setting them up!
