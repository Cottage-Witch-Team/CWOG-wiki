# Obtain a Scribe's Table

> I would explain it to you but that would de-scribe it

Craft a **Scribe's Table**{.aqua} to take your next steps. The Scribe's Table is where you will do your **magical crafting**{.green} and acquire new Glyphs!



To see the new Glyphs, **right click on the Table with your Spellbook**{.gold}. You can use the tabs on the right to **sort by tier**{.green}. Find the Glyph you'd like to craft and select it.



Once a Glyph is selected, gather the items and **throw them onto the table**{.gold}. The floating item will disappear when it is accepted by the table.



You may need **some unique items called Essences**{.green}. These are crafted on the **Imbuement Chamber**{.aqua} and will require the use of **Pedestals**{.aqua}.
