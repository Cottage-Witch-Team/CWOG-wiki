# New Potion Options

> The potion jar, melder, and flasks

The **Potion Jar**{.aqua} can hold up to 100 doses of a single potion.



You can use it to refill your **Potion Flask**{.aqua}, which is a container able to hold up to 8 doses of the same potion type.



There are two variants of the Potion Flask: **Extend Time**{.green} and **Amplify**{.green}. I think you can guess what each does.
