# Starbuncle Charm

**Starbuncles**{.aqua} are small, squirrel-like creatures which love **Gold Nuggets**{.aqua}. Find a wild Starbuncle and toss them one to receive a **Starbuncle Shard**{.aqua} in return, which is used to craft its **Charm.**{.aqua} You can simply **right click the charm on the ground**{.gold} to summon its tame counterpart.



When following you, the **Starbuncle**{.yellow} will grant you **Speed II**{.green} and, when fed Gold Nuggets, it will give you the ability to **see gold veins through blocks**{.green}.



The **Starbuncle**{.yellow} is capable of **item transport**{.green}. Use a **Dominion Wand**{.aqua} to assign it to certain inventories. It will also **harvest fully-grown**{.green} **Source Berries**{.aqua}.
