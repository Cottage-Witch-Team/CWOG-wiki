# Moving Items

**Mechanical Belts**{.aqua} convey items between machines and inventories **horizontally or diagonally**{.green}. Items placed on a **Mechanical Belt**{.aqua} will stick to it and not despawn. **Chutes**{.aqua} can transport items **vertically and diagonally**{.green}. By default they transport downward, but **hook them up to**{.gold} **Encased Fans**{.aqua} to change the direction. Unlike hoppers, they do not have a multi-slot inventory, and can transport multiple items at once.

You can learn more about how they work by **holding w to Ponder**{.gold}.
