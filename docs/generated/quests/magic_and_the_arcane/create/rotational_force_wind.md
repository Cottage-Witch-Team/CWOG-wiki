# Rotational Force: Wind

A mid-game method of generating rotational force is with a windmill. A small windmill can be made relatively easily, and can be expanded later on to generate more stress units.



A windmill requires a **Windmill Bearing**{.aqua}, which can be **placed facing any direction**{.green}. The front face of the Bearing is sticky, and **a block placed on it**{.gold} will become part of the windmill. The back of the bearing is where shafts can be connected to distribute the windmill’s rotational force. **Now you have two choices**{.pink}, using **Sails**{.aqua} or **Wools**{.aqua} as the 

 blocks.



Once you have attached 8 or more **Sails**{.aqua} or sail-like blocks, **right click the**{.gold} **Windmill Bearing**{.aqua} to activate the structure. **Hold right-click while looking at the side of bearing**{.gold} to configure the rotational direction of the windmill. If **Wools**{.aqua} are used, you have to **apply glue to attach the blocks together**{.gold} as they do not automatically connect like sails do.



You can expand a windmill up to 128 sail/sail-like blocks to generate a maximum of **8192 su**{.green} at a speed of **16 rpm**{.green}. Any additional **Sails**{.aqua} or **Wools**{.aqua} **does not generate more stress units or rotational speed**{.green}.



Other non- **Sails**{.aqua} or **Wools**{.aqua} blocks can also be attached to the windmill structure. These additional blocks **will not affect the efficiency of the windmill**{.green} so feel free to decorate the windmill as you please!
