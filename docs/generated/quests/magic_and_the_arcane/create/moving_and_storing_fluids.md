# Moving and Storing Fluids

For certain recipes you may wish to use **Fluids**{.aqua}, and for that you'll need **Tanks**{.aqua}, **Pipes**{.aqua}, and **Pumps**{.aqua}.

To test it out, why not **set up an infinite**{.pink} **Lava**{.aqua} **source**{.pink} ? Set up a basic **Lava Generator**{.green} with **Dripstone**{.aqua} and a **Cauldron**{.aqua}.

Then, place a **Pump**{.aqua} and/or some **Fluid Pipes**{.aqua} beside it, as well as a **Tank**{.aqua} to hold the liquid. Power the pump and **use the Wrench to change the Pump's direction**{.gold}.
