# Dispensing Fluids

Now that you have a **Pump and Pipes**{.aqua}, what do you do with the fluid?



Well, you have a few options. You could **compact the fluid**{.green} into solids using a **Mechanical Press**{.aqua} (see the other quests for this!), or you could use a **Spout**{.aqua} to **dispense fluid onto or into items**{.green} on a Depot.



Spouts can be used in making **Chocolate-Covered Berries**{.aqua}, **Honeyed Apples**{.aqua}, **Sweet Rolls**{.aqua}, and eventually also **Blaze Cakes**{.aqua} !
