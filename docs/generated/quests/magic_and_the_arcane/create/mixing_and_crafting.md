# Mixing and Crafting

The **Mechanical Mixer**{.aqua} is used for multiple functions such as **creating alloys**{.green} and **autocrafting shapeless recipes**{.green}.

It must be paired with a **Basin**{.aqua}, which holds the ingredients for it to mix.

Let's test it out by **making some**{.pink} **Chocolate Bars**{.aqua}.

Take these ingredients and **set up your Basin above a Heat Source**{.gold}. This should make one bucket of **Melted Chocolate**{.aqua}. You can either **extract it with a**{.gold} **Pump**{.aqua}, or with a **Bucket**{.aqua}.
