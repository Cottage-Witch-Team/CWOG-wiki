# Crow Ankh Amulet

Equip your **Crow**{.yellow} with this **Ankh**{.aqua} to protect it from danger. It will grant it **Regen**{.green} while it's playing dead, and **acts as a**{.green} **Totem of Undying**{.aqua} in case the **Crow**{.yellow} dies.
