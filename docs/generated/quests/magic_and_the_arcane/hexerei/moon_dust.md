# Moon Dust

Similar to moon water in real life, you can obtain **Moon Dust**{.aqua} by combining **Redstone**{.aqua} and **Glowstone**{.aqua} **during a Full Moon**{.gold}.



It's used for some important recipes, such high level **Storage Glyphs**{.aqua}, so it's a good idea to have some on hand! You can use a **Moon Dial**{.aqua} to tell what moon phase is currently active. You can **hold it and right click**{.gold} to see how many days remain until the full moon.
