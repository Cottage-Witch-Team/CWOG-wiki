# Crow Flute

> Utterly charming

Crows love music! If you ever lose track of them, you can instantly summon them using the **Crow Flute**{.aqua}. You'll need **Mahogany Wood**{.aqua} and some **Dye**{.aqua} in order to craft. **Right click while holding the Flute**{.gold} to play a tune to call your **Familiars**{.yellow}.



If you **crouch and right click a Crow with the Flute**{.gold}, you can Select it with the **Select**{.green} button. Then, you can select **Perch**{.green}, and **crouch and right click a block/inventory item with the Flute**{.gold} in order to tell your Crow to perch there. Crows will bring items back to their perch when told to **Gather**{.green}.
