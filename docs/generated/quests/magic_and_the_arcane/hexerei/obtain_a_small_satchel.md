# Obtain a Small Satchel

> Portable storage

You can attach a **Satchel**{.aqua} to your **Broom**{.aqua} both as a cute decoration, and as a way to store items on the go!



There are three Satchel items: **Small Satchel**{.aqua}, **Medium**{.aqua}, and **Large**{.aqua}. Only one can be equipped at any given time.



The Medium Satchel requires a Small Satchel in order to craft. The Large requires one Small and one Medium, and combines them together on your Broom.



Crouch and right-click your Broom to access its **accessories and inventory**{.green}.
