# Make A Broom and Whistle

> Any kind works!

There are three types of **Brooms**{.aqua}, **Willow**{.green}, **Witch Hazel**{.green}, and **Mahogany**{.green}. The Witch Hazel and Willow brooms are affordable to make, but slower to control and flammable. The Mahogany broom is faster and fire resistant- a worthy upgrade!



You can **crouch and right click**{.gold} your **Broom**{.aqua} in order to access its customization options, as well as switch it from **Float Mode**{.green} to **Rest Mode**{.green}, which determines what the Broom will do when you dismount.



You will also need a **Broom Whistle**{.aqua}.



Lost your broom? No worries! Just **right click**{.gold} with your **Broom Whistle**{.aqua} to call it to you!

First, you'll need to **place the**{.gold} **Whistle**{.aqua} **and**{.gold} **Broom**{.aqua} in a crafting grid to bind them. You can **unbind**{.green} them by **putting the**{.gold} **Whistle**{.aqua} in alone.



Be aware that this **works best when your broom is in sight**{.pink}. It will try to fly to you, but it's just a broom so it's **not very smart**{.green} and often gets stuck on things.
