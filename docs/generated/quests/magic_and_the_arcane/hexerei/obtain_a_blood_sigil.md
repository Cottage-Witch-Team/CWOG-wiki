# Obtain a Blood Sigil

Blood Sigils are used to collect blood for rituals. They are crafted with **Redstone**{.aqua} and **Polished Blackstone**{.aqua}.

Once you have crafted the Sigil, place it in the bottom of your **Mixing Cauldron**{.aqua} (by **right-clicking and placing it in the left slot of the crafting interface**{.green} ).

Once it is installed, make sure there is no liquid in your Cauldron, and then **jump inside of it**{.green}. Blood must be taken from a willing participant, and that means you! Jump 3 times to obtain enough Blood for 1 Bottle. Then, right click the Cauldron with a **Glass Bottle**{.aqua} to collect it.
