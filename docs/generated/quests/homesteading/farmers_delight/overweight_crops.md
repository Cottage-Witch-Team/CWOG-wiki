# Overweight Crops

> Everyone loves a chubby carrot

With this mod, crops have a small chance of spawning **giant crops**{.green}, similar to Stardew Valley. This is most likely to **happen to fully grown crops exposed to Moonlight**{.green}.



Break them with a **Hoe**{.aqua} to get significantly more regular crops than usual. Break them with an **Axe**{.aqua} or your hand to obtain the Crop Block itself. You can then **Cook**{.green} them, **Peel**{.green} them, or place them on a **Cutting Board**{.aqua} to gather the crops from them.



And you even have a chance of Overweight Crops when using **Planters**{.aqua} ! **Rich Farmland**{.aqua} has an increased chance of spawning Overweight Crops.
