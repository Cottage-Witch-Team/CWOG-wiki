# Obtain a Reaping Tool

> Don't fear the reaper...seriously

The **Reaping Tool**{.aqua}, known in some circles as a Scythe, allows you to perform a finicky sort of magic known as **Reaping**{.green}.



One **right clicks**{.gold} with the **Scythe**{.aqua} to reap an animal. If successful, you will **obtain its drops without killing it**{.green}, instead stealing its youth and transforming it into a baby. However, if you are unsuccessful, **nature takes its course**{.green}.



You can ensure **better chances of success**{.green} with **better materials**{.aqua}, with **Netherite**{.aqua} giving you a near 100% success rate. **Hover and hold shift**{.gold} over each tool to see the base failure rate.



This ritual does not work on mobs with no baby stage, such as **Cod**{.yellow}. It also does not work on **hostile mobs**{.yellow}, even if they DO have a baby stage.



If you **reap a baby animal, it does die**{.pink}, so don't be greedy! Remember, the key to sustainable agriculture is to **harvest responsibly**{.green}. **Reaping Tools**{.aqua} can be used by **Dispensers**{.aqua}.



Placing a **Reaping Tool**{.aqua} into an **Enchanting Table**{.aqua} results in **Unbreaking only, 99% of the time**{.pink}. It's better to enchant it with books. **Reaping Tools**{.aqua} can be enchanted with **Mending, Sharpness, and Unbreaking**{.green}, as you might expect.

However, they can also be enchanted with **Executioner**{.green}, which **currently has no effect**{.pink}.
