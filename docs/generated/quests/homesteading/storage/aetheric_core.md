The **Aetheric Core**{.aqua} is made using the **fairy ring**{.green}. You will need to **wait about 15 seconds**{.green} and the items will combined into your **Aetheric Core**{.aqua} ! **Exploding**{.gold} an **Aetheric Core**{.aqua} and an **Ender Dust**{.aqua} will **irreversibly entangle two**{.green} **Aetheric Fragments**{.aqua} **together**{.green}, but also put a hole in your base. **Try dropping these two items next to a**{.gold} **Tiny TNT**{.aqua} **and igniting the**{.gold} **Tiny TNT**{.aqua} **with a**{.gold} **Flint and Steel**{.aqua} **instead**{.gold} ! These **Entangled Aetheric Fragments**{.aqua} will help you gain cross dimensional storage access.



The two **Entangled Aetheric Fragments**{.aqua} you create represent **two halves of a shared connection**{.green}, **you will need both, so don't lose them**{.pink} !
