# Quantity Modifiers

The different levels of **Quantity Modifiers**{.aqua} will allow **Sculk Emitters**{.aqua} to transfer and store up to **64 items**{.green} at a time! **Receivers**{.aqua} and **Transmitters**{.aqua} cannot receive these upgrades, but will work with the items sent to them by the **Emitter**{.aqua}.
