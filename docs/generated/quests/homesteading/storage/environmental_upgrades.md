# Environmental Upgrades

> Let 'em know you're coming

These upgrades allow your backpack to operate outside the confines of its leather! **Pickup Upgrade**{.gold} will reroute any items you pick up to enter your backpack instead of your inventory. **Magnet Upgrade**{.gold} will draw items to your backpack from a distance! **Jukebox Upgrade**{.gold} will allow you to be that one hiker who always brings their bluetooth speaker with them to bless the entire trail with the soothing tunes of Nicki Minaj.



To get the most of these upgrades, we suggest **checking out the settings tab**{.gold} for each one. 



Your settings will be saved for each upgrade **even if you remove it.**{.green}
