# Biometric Card

The **Ward Bypass**{.aqua} is an item which allows you configure the behavior of your **Nexus**{.green}. To alter the **Bypass**{.aqua}, place it in the **Guardian Ward**{.aqua} and **select the appropriate permissions**{.gold}, as in the image below.



![ae2_guardian_ward.png](/assets/textures/quest_images/ae2_guardian_ward.png)



This **Bypass**{.aqua} is currently unclaimed, and if placed inside the **Guardian Ward**{.aqua}, it will apply the given permissions to anyone accessing the system. However, you can further configure access to your Nexus by having your coven **claim a Bypass**{.green} with the permissions you've set on it.



To claim a **Ward Bypass**{.aqua}, a player should **hold it in their hand and shift-click**{.gold}. Once the **Bypass**{.aqua} belongs to that player, you can **place it inside the**{.gold} **Guardian Ward**{.aqua} Bypass Editor to give that player the permissions enscrolled in that Bypass. **A quick note on Nexus owners**{.pink} - if you were the person to **place down the**{.green} **Guardian Ward**{.aqua}, you cannot deposit a claimed **Bypass**{.aqua}. This is designed to prevent witches from accidentally banning themselves from their own Nexus.
