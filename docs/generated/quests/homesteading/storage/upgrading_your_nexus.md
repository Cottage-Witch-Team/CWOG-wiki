# Upgrading Your Nexus

Every witch loves to tinker - we just can't leave well enough alone! And to support this habit, there are certain **Upgrades**{.aqua} to your **Storage Nexus**{.aqua} which can improve your overall experience.



They are deceptively simple to make, so long as you have some **Consecrated Quartz**{.aqua} (and perhaps other gems). Form these into a **Tablet**{.aqua}, and then **carve that tablet with certain components**{.gold} to create enhancements, which you can learn more about in the following quests!
