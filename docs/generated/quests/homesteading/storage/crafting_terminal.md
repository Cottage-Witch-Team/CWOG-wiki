# Crafting Terminal

Now you've got your power, and your storage. Now you just need a way to access it! Build yourself a **Scrying Nexus**{.aqua} to be able to add and remove items from your **Repository**{.aqua}. **Make sure to place it upon a cable connected to the**{.gold} **Repository**{.aqua}. Remember- **everything must have direct contact**{.green} with a cable or another part of your system.



If you'd like to be able to craft within your **Scrying Nexus**{.aqua}, you can **craft it together**{.gold} with a **Crystal Ball**{.aqua} and **Crafting Table**{.aqua}.



That's it- that's literally **all you need to know**{.green} for a basic setup. Everything else is a bonus! If you'd like to **learn more**{.green} or **connect up to your existing storage**{.green}, check out the optional quests!
