# Inventory Management

> Mary poppins got nothin' on this

As you improve your backpack, you will unlock upgrade slots. Just like with **Sophisticated Storage**{.blue}, you can use those slots to **increase stack size**{.green} with the **Stack Upgrades**{.aqua} or **automatically compact items**{.green} with the **Compacting Upgrade**{.aqua} to maximize storage space.
