# Wireless Crafting Terminal

> Akin to a bag of holding

So your storage is **wireless**{.green}, but how do you **access the contents when you're away**{.green} ? You'll need to **craft**{.gold} a **Resonant Aperture**{.aqua}, of course.



When you craft the **Resonant Aperture**{.aqua}, it will start **unattuned**{.green}. **To attune it to your nexus**{.green} you must **insert it into a**{.gold} **Guardian Ward**{.aqua}.

![ae2_guardian_ward_wireless.png](/assets/textures/quest_images/ae2_guardian_ward_wireless.png)

After you've **attuned**{.green} your **Resonant Aperture**{.aqua}, you must be within range of a **Aetheric Beacon**{.aqua} for it to work. With no upgrades, this is **limited to 16 blocks**{.pink}.



You can gain **infinite, cross dimensional access**{.green} by creating a **Planar Bridge Card**{.aqua} and a **Linked Entangled Aetheric Fragment**{.aqua} to upgrade your **Resonant Aperture**{.aqua} with **(see the next quests)**{.green}.



Over time and with use, the **Resonant Aperture**{.aqua} may **lose resonance**{.green}, and if this occurs, you can **place it in a**{.gold} **Charging Gadget**{.aqua} to fill it back up with **resonance**{.green}.
