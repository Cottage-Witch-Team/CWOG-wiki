# Stack Higher

> Higher and higher

Your Sophisticated **Barrels**{.aqua} and **Chests are capable of**{.aqua} **holding more than a stack per slot**{.green}, if you use the **Stack Upgrades**{.aqua} on them. The higher the tier, the more stacks each slot can hold.



You can also **use more than one Stack Upgrade**{.green}, and they will add **multiplicatively**{.green} !



So if you use a **Tier 1**{.aqua} and a **Tier 2**{.aqua}, you will hold 2 stacks by 4 stacks, meaning 8 stacks, or **512 items per slot**{.pink}.
