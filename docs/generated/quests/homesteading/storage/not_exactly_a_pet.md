# Not Exactly a Pet

> Om nom nom nom

There are many ways to carry items with you while you adventure, but not all of them are quite as terrifying as a **Luggage**{.aqua}. It has little concern for others, and loves to **gobble up dropped items**{.green}. And yet, it's strangely endearing.



You can **access its inventory with right click**{.gold}, and **pick it up with shift right click**{.gold}. It **retains its inventory**{.green} when picked up, and it cannot die.



The **Luggage**{.yellow} can **wear a collar**{.green}, which will grant it the enchanted abilities. However, **placing the Luggage in your inventory will delete the collar**{.pink}, so be sure to remove it before picking it back up.
