# Shortcut Upgrades

> "i don't like to do anything" - docm77

Don't you get tired of having to **open your backpack to get things**{.green} ? Isn't it annoying to have to **put food in your mouth**{.green} every time you're hungry? Who has time to **switch between their axe and pickaxe**{.green} when harvesting materials?



Get yourself a backpack that does it all with these upgrades. No more wasted time **grabbing the next stack of blocks**{.green} or even **opening your backpack to refill it.**{.green} You're busy and have more important matters to attend to.



To get the most of these upgrades, we suggest **checking out the settings tab**{.gold} for each one. 



Your settings will be saved for each upgrade **even if you remove it.**{.green}
