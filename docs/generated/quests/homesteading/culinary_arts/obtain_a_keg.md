# Obtain a Keg

To get your brewery started, you'll first need a **Keg**{.aqua}, and some **Tankards**{.aqua}. Place the Tankards into the Keg with some **Water**{.aqua} and **Ingredients**{.green}, such as Wheat for Beer, or Honey for Mead.
