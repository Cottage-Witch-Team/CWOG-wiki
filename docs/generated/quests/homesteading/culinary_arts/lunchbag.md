# Lunchbag

> Bring lunch with you!

A **lunchbag**{.aqua}, upgradeable to an iron or gold lunchbox, is a handy-dandy way to carry a variety of foods with you wherever you go. You can **eat the lunchbox directly**{.green} for a randomised snack from its interior- an excellent way to easily **increase your food variety score**{.green}, which increases your hearts!



A word of warning... don't trust seagulls. They'll swoop down and **steal food right out of your inventory**{.green} ; even your whole lunchbox! **Caution: entering Creative Mode with the lunchbox in your inventory will cause its contents to be wiped.**{.pink}
