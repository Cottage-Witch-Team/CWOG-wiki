# Obtain a Cooking Pot

> Despite the name, it's not for cooking pot.

The **Cooking Pot**{.aqua} will allow you to make uniquely filling and delicious dishes! The more different foods you try, the **more hearts you'll gain**{.green}, making this an important tool for the kitchen witch adventurer.



Place the **Cooking Pot**{.aqua} on top of a heat source such as **fire**{.aqua}, **campfire**{.aqua}, **magma block**{.aqua}, or **stove**{.aqua}, to heat it. Then, **place your ingredients inside**{.gold}.



You'll also need something to serve your dish in, such as a **bowl**{.aqua}, **bottle**{.aqua}, or a **pumpkin**{.aqua} !



When the **Cooking Pot**{.aqua} is broken, it retains its contents.
