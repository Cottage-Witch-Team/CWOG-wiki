# Create a Stove

> Steve's stove

A **Stove**{.aqua} is an incredibly useful tool. Thanks to your inherent magic, it is fueled at all times! You can **place up to 6 food items**{.gold} on the grill in order to cook them. They'll pop up into items when they're ready! You can **place an upside-down**{.gold} **Basket**{.aqua} above it to collect them automatically.



The **Stove**{.aqua} can also be used as a heat source for the **Cooking Pot**{.aqua}, but if you do it that way, you cannot place items on the grill.
