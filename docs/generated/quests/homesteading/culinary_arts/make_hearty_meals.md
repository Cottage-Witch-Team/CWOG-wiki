# Make Hearty Meals

> Touched by his noodly appendage.

You can make **Pasta with Meatballs**{.aqua} in a **Cooking Pot**{.aqua}. Eating Full-Course meals, such as this one, will grant you the **Nourishing**{.green} effect, which **prevents you from losing hunger and saturation**{.green} for a time. Helpful if you'll be going somewhere dangerous, as it will keep your health regenerating!
