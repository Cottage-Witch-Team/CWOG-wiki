# Guide Book

**Caupona**{.blue} is a cooking mod that employs a freeform style of recipe.

With most recipes, you will **maintain a ratio**{.green} of ingredients, such as ensuring that you have **"mostly beef"**{.green} and **"some vegetables"**{.green}, with **"no fish"**{.green}, and so on.

You can use **the guidebook**{.aqua} or **JEI**{.blue} to learn the recipes.

![cauponajei.png](/assets/textures/cauponajei.png)

When viewing **Stews and Soups**{.aqua}, ensure that you **click these icons**{.gold} to access their recipes.



![cauponarecipe.png](/assets/textures/cauponarecipe.png)

A) This is the **Soup Base**{.aqua}. For example, for **Nail Soup**{.aqua}, you **boil water**{.gold}.

B) This is the **ratio of ingredients to liquid**{.green}. For example, 3 or more ingredients to 1 bucket/4 bowls of liquid.

C) This is the **output**{.green}, or what the recipe will make.

D) These are ingredients which **must not be included**{.green}.

E and F) These are the **ratio of items to each other**{.green}, for example, a majority of **beef**{.aqua} and at least one **vegetable**{.aqua}.



Now that you know how to read the recipes, let's make some!
