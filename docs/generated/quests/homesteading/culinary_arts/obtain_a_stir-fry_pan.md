# Obtain a Stir-Fry Pan

To stir-fry things, you'll need two things: a **pan or stone**{.aqua}, and a **Gravy Boat**{.aqua}.



The Pan must be **placed atop a heat source**{.gold}. The **Gravy Boat**{.aqua} must be placed **directly next to the pan**{.green}. You can place it with **shift right click**{.gold}.
