# Bring Them To Life

> Wake them up inside

Where are all the fish...? **Aquaculture**{.blue} fish **do not spawn in the world**{.green}, you can only **fish them up**{.gold}. 



Don't worry though, they aren't dead- only sleeping! **Combine that fish with a water bucket**{.gold} to wake them right up.



If you'd like something with a bit more pizazz, you can **strike a dead fish with lightning**{.gold} to get its heart pumping. Make sure you have a bucket ready though- or you **may end up with a cooked fish**{.pink} !
