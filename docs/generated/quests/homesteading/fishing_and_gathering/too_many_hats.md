# Too Many Hats!

> I'm down to my ankles in hats

Maybe you're feeling overencumbered with hats... or perhaps you just have a hat you **really hate**{.italic}. Time to clean up your storage space and **upcycle those lids**{.green} ! **Place the**{.gold} **hat**{.aqua} **and**{.gold} **a pair of shears**{.aqua} **into a crafting grid**{.gold}. This will give you a **Hat Scrap**{.aqua}. You can do two things with that hat scrap: **Combine 4 of them**{.gold} in a crafting menu to get a **new hatbag**{.aqua} of that type:

![hatscrap_bag.png](/assets/textures/hatscrap_bag.png)



Or **combine 2 of them**{.gold} to get 1 **upgraded**{.italic} **hat scrap**{.aqua}.

![hatscrap_upgrade.png](/assets/textures/hatscrap_upgrade.png)



Unfortunately, **Epic Hats**{.pink} are not able to be broken down as they are too epic.
