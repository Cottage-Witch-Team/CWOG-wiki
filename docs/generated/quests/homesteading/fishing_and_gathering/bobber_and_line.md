# Bobber and Line

> Tools of the trade

You can craft a **Bobber**{.aqua} and **Fishing Line**{.aqua} to customize your **Fishing Rod**{.aqua} ! These can be dyed any color, and are a cosmetic only change.



note: the **Fishing Line**{.aqua} and **Unbreakable Fishing Line**{.aqua} are unrelated items. The latter is a meant to be **equipped in your Curios**{.gold}.
