# Gadgets, Part 1

> The building gadget and exchanging gadget

The **Building Gadget**{.aqua} is a useful tool for building larger structures quickly. It **places multiple blocks at once**{.green} **from your inventory.**{.green} **Shift right click a block in world**{.gold} to set that as your building block.



The **Exchanging Gadget**{.aqua} is a tool which will **replace blocks in the world with a specific block from your inventory**{.green}. As with the BG, **shift right click the replacer block**{.gold} and then you can **right click with the gadget**{.gold}.
