# Obtain a Sign Post

> Show the way

Up to two **Sign Posts**{.aqua} can be placed on any fence block, in order to form directional signs! **Shift and right click**{.gold} the placed Sign in order to reverse the direction.



If you **right click them with a compass**{.gold}, they will point in the direction the compass points to. Useful for spawn points and Lodestones!
