# Handcraft your Home

To begin with the furniture in **Handcrafted**{.blue}, you'll likely need some **Wooden Boards**{.aqua}. These can be easily obtained by **placing planks in a**{.gold} **Stonecutter**{.aqua} or **Create**{.blue} **Saw**{.aqua}.



You can **right click the**{.gold} **Hammer**{.aqua} on Handcrafted blocks to **change their appearance**{.green}.
