# The Overcast Columns

According to the ancient texts:



The **Overcast Columns**{.red} are a vast, uninhabited wasteland. They are composed of multiple amber columns, which have a biome-exclusive **Crystallized Amber**{.aqua} block in each of them. Overcast Columns also have multiple **Geysers**{.aqua} scattered throughout the terrain, which is composed of **Gloomslate**{.aqua} and **Gloom Sculk**{.aqua}. Nothing is known to live here due to the **hostile and barren nature**{.green} of the environment.
