# Making the Sculk Sensor

> Potentially helpful for automation

**Sculk Sensors**{.aqua} are cute - and mildly terrifying - beings which will **transmit a redstone signal when alerted**{.green}. They listen for nearby sounds, such as **footsteps, blocks placing or breaking, or opening inventories**{.green}.



You can **block their listening with wool**{.gold}, directionally, or **surround them with wool**{.gold} to deactivate them.
