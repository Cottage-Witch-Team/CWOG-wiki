# Discover the Ancient Temple

Within the **Otherside Deeplands**{.red}, one can find an **Ancient Temple**{.green}. These structures are filled with the **spoils of those who came before**{.green}.



In the **center of the temple**{.pink}, there are some blocks which, when broken, allow you to **descend further into the Temple**{.gold}.
