# The Otherside Deeplands

According to the ancient texts:



The first biome is called **Otherside Deeplands**{.red}. The Deeplands are made up of mostly **Sculk**{.aqua} and **Sculk Stone**{.aqua}. Large groups of **Phantoms**{.yellow} can be seen flying around the caverns of this biome, along with hordes of **Sculk Snappers**{.yellow}. **Sculk Jaws**{.aqua} and **Infested Sculk**{.aqua} are very common in the Deeplands, making it more **difficult to navigate**{.green}. Beware of the **Shriekworms**{.yellow} which lay beneath the **Infested Sculk**{.aqua}, waiting to strike.



The **Warden**{.yellow} can be summoned here, as well.
