# Explore the Otherside

The **Otherside**{.red} is a dark and dangerous place. It's likely you **won't be able to see**{.green} more than a few feet ahead of you.

Thankfully, the **Warden Helmet**{.aqua} will alleviate this issue.

Within the Otherside are **three different biomes**{.green}, each rather unwelcoming.
