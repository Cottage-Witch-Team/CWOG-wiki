# Soul Elytra

Obtain **Soul Dust**{.aqua} **from the**{.pink} **Leechers**{.yellow} and **Snappers**{.yellow}, a **Soul Crystal**{.aqua} from defeating **Stalkers**{.yellow}, and **Sculk Bones**{.aqua} from defeated **Sculk Centipedes**{.yellow}.



Once you have these ingredients, you can convert your **Elytra**{.aqua} to a **Soul Elytra**{.aqua}.
