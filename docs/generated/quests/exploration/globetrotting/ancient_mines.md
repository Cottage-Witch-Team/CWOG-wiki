# Ancient Mines

The **Ancient Mines**{.red} been to be seen to be believed. A massive underground chasm, once inhabited by those who delved too greedily and too deep. Beware what now lives there. **CW: Giant Cave Centipedes**{.pink}. Although they are disabled in most of the modpack, this structure has a spawner for them. Be prepared, should you go inside.
