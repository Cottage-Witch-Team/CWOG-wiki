# Tadpole In A Bucket

**Tadpoles**{.yellow}, otherwise known as "the forbidden boba," are cute lil wiggly fellas who are particularly susceptible to **Axolotls**{.yellow}.

They're also susceptible to **Buckets**{.aqua}, and if you should capture one in such a way, then you've completed this quest.
