# Guster Eye

The **Guster**{.yellow} is an elemental spirit which can appear during storms. It will throw nearby entities or items up into the air, and are **resistant to arrows**{.green}.

Once defeated, they can drop their **Eyes**{.aqua}, which can be used either to make a **Pocket of Sand**{.aqua}, to throw in the face of your enemies - or the **Gustmaker**{.aqua}, a block which acts as a fan when provided with a Redstone signal.
