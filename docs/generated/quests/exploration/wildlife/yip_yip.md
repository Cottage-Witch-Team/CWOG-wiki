# Yip Yip!

> Better than carbuncle

Craft a **Summoning Staff**{.aqua} to bind to your mount ( **right click them with the staff!**{.gold} ). This will allow you to command them to sit, follow, or wander... and summon them to your side from anywhere! Be warned though: **only one mount can be bound to a staff**{.green}.
