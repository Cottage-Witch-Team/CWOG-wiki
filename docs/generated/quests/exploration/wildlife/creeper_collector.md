# Creeper Collector

> Kill one of each creeper type

Although they may not all be hostile, it would still be pretty cool if you killed one of every type of **Creeper**{.yellow}. It's like a world tour, but with murder!



As a reward, here's a **particularly rare creeper**{.yellow} that doesn't spawn in the world... maybe keep it in an aquarium as a trophy?



Note: As some creepers may be harder to find than others, you may choose to **tackle them all at once**{.green} using a **Creeper Gate Pearl**{.aqua}. But be warned- **if a single creeper blows up**{.pink}, the gate will collapse. Reach the final wave to meet and defeat every creeper!
