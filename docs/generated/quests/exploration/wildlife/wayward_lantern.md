# Wayward Lantern

The **Wayward Lantern**{.aqua} will allow any lost pets to **find their way home**{.green}. Place it near your base, and if you should lose your pet while exploring, watch for them there.

If you **lose your pet in another dimension**{.green}, you will need to **place a Lantern in that dimension**{.gold} to get them back.



In **Multiplayer**{.pink}, your pets will respawn at the **closest Lantern**{.green}, so be careful in other people's areas.
