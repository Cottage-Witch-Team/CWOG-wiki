# Froststalker Helmet

The **Froststalker**{.aqua} helmet is worn to convince **Froststalkers**{.yellow} that the player is one of them.

It must be crafted from one of their horns. Once it is worn, you can **breed them with pork**{.gold} - but be warned, taking off the helmet will **make the babies hostile as well**{.pink}.
