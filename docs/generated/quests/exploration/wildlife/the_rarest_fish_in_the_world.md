# The Rarest Fish in the World

> Speak of the devil...

The **Devil's Hole Pupfish**{.green} is the rarest being in the world, spawning in only a single chunk - EVER - in any given world.

Find one with a **Strange Fish Locator**{.aqua}. Or, you know, get extremely lucky.

The ponds they spawn in are usually underground. These fish love to nibble on **Moss Blocks**{.aqua}, and will sometimes breed after doing so!
