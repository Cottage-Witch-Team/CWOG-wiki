# Deed Of Ownership

The **Deed of Ownership**{.aqua} is an item used in **multiplayer**{.green}. You can **right click it on an animal**{.gold} and then give the **Deed**{.aqua} to a friend. When they use it, the pet will now be linked to them, **as if they had tamed it themself**{.green}.



You can learn that the **Deed of Ownership**{.aqua} is only obtainable by **trading with an animal tamer villager**{.green} by **left clicking**{.gold} on the **Deed of Ownership**{.aqua} in **JEI**{.blue}
