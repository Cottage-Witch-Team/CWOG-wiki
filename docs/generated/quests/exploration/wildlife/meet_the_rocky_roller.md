# Meet the Rocky Roller

> I don't give a damn about my reputation

The **Rocky Roller**{.yellow} dwells in Dripstone Caves and will roll at its prey to crush them. It is weak but the strong armor on its back will protect it from most blows.

If it drops its **Rocky Hide**{.aqua}, this can be crafted into a chestplate which allows the player to **roll into mobs**{.green} and damage them while sprinting. It will also cause the player to **sink in water**{.green}.
