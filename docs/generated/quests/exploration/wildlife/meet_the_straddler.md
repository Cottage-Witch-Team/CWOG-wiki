# Meet the Straddler

> Just gonna throw my baby at you real quick

The **Straddler**{.yellow} is a bizarre and hostile creature who will throw their young at you to do damage. For the most part, defeating them yields **Basalt**{.aqua}, however they can rarely drop **Straddlite**{.aqua}. **Straddlite**{.aqua} is a material which can be crafted into a Straddleboard, an enchantable board which can **surf quickly across lava**{.green}. This is an improvement over the **Obsidian Boat**{.aqua} due to its speed, enchantability, and ability to jump!

And, as a side note, the **Stradpoles**{.yellow} can be nabbed in a bucket and also **survive in water**{.green}.
