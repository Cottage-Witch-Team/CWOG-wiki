# Visit: Dark Tower

> I ain't afraid of no ghast

In the Center of the **Dark Forest**{.pink}, the Dark Tower can be found. It is a massive structure, filled with monsters, and ruled over by a deadly **Ur-Ghast**{.yellow}.

There are four smaller turrets, each containing a **Tower Key**{.aqua}. These keys are used to unlock the central tower. There are many challenges here to undergo, so be prepared for a long expedition.

To defeat the **Ur-Ghast**{.yellow}, you must defeat his **Carminite Ghastlings**{.yellow} in the nearby rooms, near the Ghast Traps. Then and when the beast draws near, activate the trap.

The Ur-Ghast is one of the 3 bosses you must defeat to access the **Highlands**{.pink}.
