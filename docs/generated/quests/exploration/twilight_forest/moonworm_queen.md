# Moonworm Queen

> You might want to sit down for this one

**The Moonworm Queen**{.aqua} is an...item...that is filled with Moonworms. When you **right click her**{.gold}, they will be dispensed - either directly onto a surface, or shot like an arrow. The moonworms serve no purpose other than **as a light source**{.green}. You can replenish the Queen by crafting her with **Torchberries**{.aqua}, and get...even more worms. Yay.
