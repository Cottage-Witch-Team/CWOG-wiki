# Visit: Hydra Lair

> Remove one head, and two appear!

The **Hydra**{.yellow} is a fearsome foe that is only weak in one place: within its fire-spewing maw!

It is best to take on this fight with **Fire Resistance**{.green}, and make sure you **im attacks at the Hydra's open mouths**{.green} ! You can use a **ranged weapon**{.aqua}, or use a sword to **deflect their bombs**{.green} back at them.

Once you have defeated the beast, you have completed one task which will let you challenge the Highlands.
