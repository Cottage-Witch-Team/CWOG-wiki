# Obtain a Portal Key

> Throw it in a limpid pool and then back away

**The Twilight Forest**{.pink} is a night-ridden dimension filled with mysteries and odd creatures both friendly and hostile. Freely traveling the dimension is difficult - you must conquer each challenge in turn in order to traverse.

To **make the portal**{.green}, you must **dig a 3x3x1 (or 2x2x1) hole and fill it with water**{.gold}. Then, **surround the pond with flowers or mushrooms**{.gold}. Finally, **throw a Diamond into the pool**{.gold}. Beware, opening a portal can be dangerous!
