# Visit: Knight Stronghold

> Better bring some extra torches

Located in the gloomy depths of the **Dark Forest**{.pink}, the Knight Stronghold is infested with **Goblins**{.yellow}, and you cannot enter without one of the **Trophies**{.aqua} from the previous bosses.

You can use **Maze Maps**{.aqua} here, but the structures are so massive that you may need a number of them.

It is recommended to **mark your path with torches**{.gold} to ensure that you don't double back by mistake.

The end of this dungeon contains a fight against the **Knight Phantoms**{.yellow}. These will not follow you out of the room, and can swarm you if you simply charge in. It is recommended to **retreat out to the hallway periodically if healing is needed**{.gold}.

Once you defeat them, you can now challenge the **Dark Tower**{.pink}.
