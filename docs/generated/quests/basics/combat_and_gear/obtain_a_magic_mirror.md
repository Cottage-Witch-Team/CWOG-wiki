# Obtain a Magic Mirror

> Mirror, mirror, on the wall

This **Mirror**{.aqua} is no ordinary piece of glass or silver. In addition to reflecting the nearest player to it, you can modify what it shows.



If you **right click it**{.gold} with an **Armor Stand**{.aqua}, you can use it to **view sets of armor**{.green}.



Add armor by **right clicking**{.gold} the mirror while holding a piece of armor that the **reflection is not already wearing**{.pink}. If it's wearing a helmet, and you add a helmet, it will **equip the reflection's gear to you**{.green} and add the second helmet to the reflection.



![magicmirror.png](/assets/textures/quest_images/magicmirror.png) **Right click with empty hand**{.gold} to switch armor sets. If you accidentally store more than one set of armor, you can **cycle through these by shift-clicking**{.gold}.



You can also **right click the mirror**{.gold} with a **Banner**{.aqua} to apply the banner pattern to the **background**{.green}.



And, if you'd like a more spooky option, **right click it**{.gold} with a **Skull**{.aqua} to change the preview to a **Skeleton!**{.yellow}.



All the upgrades **can be combined**{.green}.
