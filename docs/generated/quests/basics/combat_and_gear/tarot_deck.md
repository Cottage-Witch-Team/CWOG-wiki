# Tarot Deck

> What a big deck you have

Although it is difficult to make, the **Tarot Deck**{.aqua} will allow you to hold **all your cards in a single slot**{.green}, when **equipped as a Curio**{.green}. **Caution: entering Creative Mode with the Tarot Deck in your inventory will empty the deck. This error does not occur when equipped to a curio slot.**{.pink}
