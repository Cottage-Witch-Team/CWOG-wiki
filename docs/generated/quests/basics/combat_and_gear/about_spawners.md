# About Spawners

> A different kind of farming

**Spawners**{.aqua} can now be collected with Silk Touch! The **Spawner Key**{.aqua} will allow you to right click a **Spawner**{.aqua} and alter its functionality to **increase range or spawns**{.green}, etc.



By default, any **Spawners**{.aqua} you pick up **will be empty**{.green}. You'll need to **right click them with a spawn egg**{.gold} to assign them a mob. However, spawners collected via the **Create**{.blue} **Cart Assembler**{.aqua} will keep their target.
