# Heal Spell Book 1

> Why do cactus need totems of undying?

This **Healing Tome**{.aqua} was created by a shaman of the **Cactem**{.yellow}, a desert-dwelling plantlike species which have a love for **Totems of Undying**{.aqua}.



Trade them Totems for a chance at obtaining this reusable spell book. It's a rare chance, but if you have the Totems to spare...



Best of all, if you obtain enough Tomes, you can **combine them for a more powerful heal**{.gold} ! The books can go up to level 3.
