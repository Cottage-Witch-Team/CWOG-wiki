# Extreme Sound Muffler

> Selective hearing. literally.

This modpack adds a lot of new sounds. So to help you filter the ones you don't like, it also includes **Super Sound Muffler**{.green}. **Press F6 to open the menu**{.gold}. From here, you can click **Recent**{.green} to see what you've recently heard. You can play the sound to confirm it's the one you don't like, and then **mute it!**{.gold}
