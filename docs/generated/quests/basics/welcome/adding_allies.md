# Adding Allies

FTB Teams allows you to add other players to your party, which can allow you to **share quest progress**{.green} if desired, and also **allow access to your claimed chunks**{.green}.



The Teams screen is accessible in the **top left of your inventory**{.red}. From there, you can **create a party**{.green}. You can invite others if you want to share quest progress, otherwise you can simply add other players as allies via the **Manage Allies**{.aqua} screen.



![ftbmenu.png](/assets/textures/quest_images/ftbmenu.png)



![createaparty.png](/assets/textures/quest_images/createaparty.png)



![manageallies.png](/assets/textures/quest_images/manageallies.png)



Note that **in order for Deployers and such to work in claimed chunks**{.pink}, you have to **allow Fake Player access**{.gold} via FTB Teams. Press E and click on "My Team" in the top left. Then click the gear in the top right, and you'll see this menu:



![fakeplayers.png](/assets/textures/quest_images/fakeplayers.png)



Set "Allow All Fake Players" to true.
