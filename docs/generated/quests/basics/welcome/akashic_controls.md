# Akashic Controls

To read a book in your **Akashic Tome**{.aqua}, **right click**{.gold} while holding the book and **select the book you want**{.gold}.



To **return the Akashic Tome**{.green} to its regular form, **hold it and left click in the air**{.gold}.



To **remove a book from the tome**{.green}, you need to **crouch and throw**{.gold} the item (default keybind is Q).



To **add a book**{.green}, you should **craft the book with the**{.gold} **Tome**{.aqua}.
