# Cage

> Despite all my rage, i am still just a...oh you know

The **Cage**{.aqua} is similar to the **Capture Cell**{.aqua}, in that you can **right click it on a small mob**{.gold} to pick it up and transport it. You can **right click a block to release**{.gold} the mob, or you can **shift right click**{.gold} to place the **Cage**{.aqua} down and display your new friend in their enclosure!
