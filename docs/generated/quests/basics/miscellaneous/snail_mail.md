# Snail Mail

> A way to communicate in multiplayer

You can send letters and packages to your friends! You can **send and receive mail**{.green} using a **Snailbox**{.aqua}. Placing it down claims it, and you can even give it a name by **right clicking on it**{.gold} and entering text in the name field.

To **send mail**{.green}, you'll need a **Stamp**{.aqua} and an **Open Envelope**{.aqua}. These envelopes can be reused, so they're good for the environment. **Place the Envelope in the upper right of the**{.gold} **Snailbox**{.aqua}. Then click **"Open Envelope**{.gold}."

![snailmail1.png](/assets/textures/quest_images/snailmail1.png)

In here, you can put your mail! You can send **postcards, wrapped parcels, and more**{.aqua} ! **Don't forget to add a Stamp**{.green} ! Enter the name of the recipient, your name if desired, and then **Hit Esc to go back to the previous window**{.gold} !

![snailmail2.png](/assets/textures/quest_images/snailmail2.png)

Once you've hit **Esc**{.pink}, you can **select the Snailbox to send to**{.green}, and ta-da! You're done!
