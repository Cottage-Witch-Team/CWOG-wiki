# Save your EXP

> Unfortunately not the experience you can put on your resume

A piece of **Carved Nephrite**{.aqua} allows you to store your Experience in a network, to prevent it being lost or reduced on death. Connect multiple **Carved Nephrite**{.aqua} together to store even more.



It also allows you to **share experience with others**{.green} !



Additionally, you can store up to 30 levels in a **Nephrite Ring**{.aqua}.



Stand on the **Siphon**{.aqua} to drain your EXP, and activate the **Fountain**{.aqua} to shower yourself in it.
