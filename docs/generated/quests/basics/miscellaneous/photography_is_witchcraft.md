# Photography is Witchcraft

There have long been superstitions about whether **photography holds magical sway**{.green} in our world. Some say photographs steal souls - others say they grant a form of immortality.



Whatever you believe the power of the picture truly is, it cannot be denied that it **is at least somewhat magical**{.green}. The ability to capture light from a moment in time and look back on it long since it has passed? That's a small miracle without a doubt.



You, too, can **capture photos with a camera**{.green} ! **Right click**{.gold} the camera to enter photo mode. From here, you can **crouch**{.gold} to access the **photo settings**{.green} - Focal Length, Shutter Speed, Flash Mode, and Composition Guides.



But first, you'll need some **Film**{.aqua}, and you can also upgrade your camera with **other items**{.green} !
