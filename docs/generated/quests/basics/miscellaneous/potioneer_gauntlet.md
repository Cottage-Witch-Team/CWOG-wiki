# Potioneer Gauntlet

The **Potioneer's Gauntlet**{.aqua} allows one to **inject potions into their attacks**{.green} using the **Potion Injector**{.aqua}. Up to 6 **Lingering Potions**{.aqua} can be injected.



When attacking with the Gauntlet, you will deal both melee damage and the potion effect's damage. This can be toggled by pressing **shift+right click**{.gold}.



Once one potion is used, it will automatically load up the next potion after a few seconds.



They will **load in the order they were placed**{.green} in the Injector UI. The potions are not consumed, but will be on cooldown after use.



To resupply your gauntlet, apply **Blaze Powder**{.aqua} in the **Potion Injector**{.aqua}.
