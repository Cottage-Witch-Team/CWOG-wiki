# Printing Photos

You **print your photographs**{.green} using the **Lightroom**{.aqua}. Here you can **view your developed negatives**{.green} in detail, as well. **Black and White Film**{.aqua} requires **Paper**{.aqua} and **Black Dye**{.aqua}. Printing in **color**{.green} additionally requires **Cyan**{.aqua}, **Magenta**{.aqua}, and **Yellow Dyes**{.aqua}.



Once you have **chosen your photo with left click**{.gold}, you can then **click the checkmark**{.gold} to begin printing. And ta-da! Photographs!
