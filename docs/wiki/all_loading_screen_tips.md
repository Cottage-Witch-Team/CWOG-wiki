# All loading screen tips!

> You can click Advancements in chat to bring them up in the Advancements screen. You can also display your achieved Advancements using the Advancement Frame.


> The Akashic Tome has confusing controls. Right click it to select a book from the menu. Left click it in the air to reset it from a selected book. Crouch and throw the Tome when a book is selected to remove that book from it. Craft the Tome with a book to hold the book inside it.


> You can now harvest Budding Amethyst with Silk Touch!


> You can restore Anvil durability by right clicking it with an iron ingot.


> Blaze Rods are now placeable, just like End Rods!


> If a hay bale is placed underneath a Campfire, it will grant you a height boost when you fly over it with an Elytra.


> You can transport items and animals using Astikor's Animal and Supply Carts!


> Shift+t when hovering over an item in your inventory will share that item in chat for others to check out.


> Rotten Flesh and Poisonous Potatoes can be placed in the Composter.


> Both Elytra and Totems of Undying can be equipped as Curios (worn accessories, separate from armor). Press J by default to access your Curios inventory.


> Dispensers can now place blocks and seeds!


> In your Controls menu, you can bind your favourite Emotes to a key.


> You can reroll enchantments on a specific item by lifting it out of the Enchanting Table and placing it back in.


> Rarely, one can find Fairy Rings in Forest or Plains biomes. Dig down through the center of these to find Diamonds or Emeralds!


> Item frames can be colored using Dyes!


> Ghasts will sometimes cry tears without being slain, allowing you to collect them without fighting.


> Falling gravel will now be automatically mined, without the need to place torches at the bottom!


> Hoes can harvest crops and grass in a 3x3 or 5x5 area.


> You no longer have to be holding an arrow for an Infinity Bow to work.


> Hold shift and drag click to deposit multiple things quickly, or hold space and click to transfer an entire inventory back and forth.


> Press the middle mouse button in an inventory to sort it. You can also use the buttons on the sides of inventory UIs to sort, withdraw, and deposit.


> You can now place items on Lilypads.


> Dropped items will be highlighted with a colored beam of light, to help you find them.


> In multiplayer: Chest and Barrel loot is PER PERSON, meaning that if you leave a looted chest intact, another user can get their own stuff from it. Looted chests and barrels will change their textures from gold to silver.


> You can configure mods while in-game by pressing the backslash key. Note that some changes may require a restart.


> Frequently forget what you were doing? Press N to take notes! You can even pin them, and they'll show up on the side of your screen, like a sticky note!


> Obsidian boats can float across lava lakes!


> This modpack supports shaders by default, without using OptiFine! This is thanks to the Oculus mod. I recommend Complementary Shaders, by EminGTR.


> You can cycle through paintings by right clicking one on the wall, while holding one in your hand.


> Pistons can now push tile entities like Chests, even if they have something in their inventory. They cannot push spawners, however.


> You can stunt the growth of an animal by feeding it a Poisonous Potato. Have Forever Kittens! All you needed to do was poison a baby.


> There are multiple ways to prevent hostile spawns in an area. You can burn Sage on a Sage Burning Plate, or you can craft a Magnum Torch and place it down!


> Press - to access your Quests without using the Quest Book. These are designed to guide you through the mods in the pack, and reward you while doing so!


> You can harvest drops from animals without killing them, by either setting up a Drygmy in your farm, or by using a Reaping tool.


> Having trouble placing directional blocks like stairs or pistons? You can ensure they all point in your desired direction with Rotation Lock! Simply hold shift and press K while hovering over the desired face of the block. Pressing K by itself will toggle this rotation lock. It can also be used to choose whether the block is placed in the upper or lower half of the space, for easy slabs or upside-down stairs.


> New Shulkers can spawn if an Endermite burrows into a Purpur block.


> Shulker boxes preview their contents when hovered over. Right click an item on a Shulker box in your inventory to place the item easily inside.


> Right click on a Sign to edit the text.


> You can place candles on skulls to be ULTRA GOTH.


> Sugarcane grows faster when planted on Snad! That's not a typo. It's called snad.


> You can mute any sound in the game with Extreme Sound Muffler. By default, press F6 to access its menu.


> Using Shears on a Hay Bale will make a Thatch block.


> Time in a Bottle is an item used to speed up progress on things like Furnaces, Compost, and even Cheese wheels! It accumulates time while held in your inventory or placed in a Curio slot.


> You can break multiple similar blocks at once by holding the ` key as you mine.


> Villagers will follow you if you are holding an emerald block.


> If your server operator has it set up, you can now use proximity voice chat! Press 'v' by default to change your settings.


> Try giving your aquatic friends a Slime Ball! For some, such as the Blobfish or Mimic Octopus, it will allow them to survive on land!
