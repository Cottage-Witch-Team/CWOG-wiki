# Ars Nouveau Quests


---
# Can you be ars'd?
To begin your journey as an Ars Novice, you must first obtain the **Worn Notebook**. This can be found in your **Akashic Tome**, by right clicking and choosing the right book.

To return your Akashic Tome to its base state, **hold it and left click** while **targeting the air**.

---
# Obtain a Novice Spell Book
To obtain the **Novice Spell Book**, you must **remove the Worn Notebook** and craft it with **Gold** in a **Crafting Table**.

To retrieve your **Worn Notebook** from your **Akashic Tome**, **select the Worn Notebook in the tome**, then **crouch and throw the book**. This will eject the Worn Notebook so you can craft with it.

The name will look funny. That's normal, you can craft with it just fine.

Once you have your **Novice Spell Book**, you can **hold it and press c** to access the interface.
The top tab is **the worn notebook, a reference guide for the mod**. The next tab lets you **build and save spells**. We'll address the rest later.

Note that **while holding your book, you cannot use the ` key**.

---
# Tick the box when you're ready
To make a spell, you must choose the **Form** (touch, self, or projectile) and the **Effect** (harm or break). These are called **Glyphs**, and you'll unlock more as you go.

For example, you could **choose Projectile and Break** to have a remote way of breaking blocks. Input the **spell name** in the bottom left, and click **Create** in the bottom right. It will save to your **first spell slot** (the 10 tabs on the right are your **Spell Slots**.

**Cast your spells** by holding your Spellbook in your main or offhand, and right click. **Select your spells with v**.

---
You can **upgrade your spell book** in order to **learn higher tiers of glyphs**.

---
The ultimate spell book! Become an **Archmage** and learn Tier 3 Glyphs!

# Obtain Archwood and Make an Amethyst Dowsing Rod
Your next step is to obtain **Archwood** of some type. These trees are found somewhat rarely in many biomes.

From the Archwood, you can craft the **Amethyst Dowsing Rod**, which will help you find **Amethyst Geodes**. This is an important magical material, so collect lots of it!

---
# Obtaining Source
Magic such as **Imbuement** requires **Source**, a force which flows through all living things. You may craft a **Sourcelink** to collect it, and a **Source Jar** to contain it,

But where does Source come from? The natural world. One way you can obtain it is via an **Agronomic Sourcelink**. **Place your Sourcelink and Jar within 6 blocks** of your livestock or garden, and it will slowly collect. **Bonemealed crops will not contribute to Source production**.

---
# Make an Imbuement Chamber
The **Imbuement Chamber** is how you can create **Source Gems**. Simply **place an Amethyst or Lapis** inside the Chamber and wait for it to slowly transform.

This can be sped up later by **giving it access to Source**, such as that contained in a **Source Jar**.

---
# Obtain a Scribe's Table
Craft a **Scribe's Table** to take your next steps. The Scribe's Table is where you will do your **magical crafting** and acquire new Glyphs!

To see the new Glyphs, **right click on the Table with your Spellbook**. You can use the tabs on the right to **sort by tier**. Find the Glyph you'd like to craft and select it.

Once a Glyph is selected, gather the items and **throw them onto the table**. The floating item will disappear when it is accepted by the table.

You may need **some unique items called Essences**. These are crafted on the **Imbuement Chamber** and will require the use of **Pedestals**.

---
# Make a Spell Parchment
**Spell Parchment** is a way for you to store a spell on paper. They are **single-use**, and can be printed at the **Scribe's Table**.

First, make a **Blank Parchment** with Paper and **Magebloom Fiber**. Then, combine that with **Source Gems** to create the final product.

Once you have placed your Scroll on the Scribe's Table, **set your desired spell in your Spellbook** and then **shift right click your Spellbook on the Table**. This will inscribe the spell onto the Parchment for you!

---
# Make some Arcane Pedestals
Craft an **Arcane Pedestal** in a Crafting Bench using **Sourcestone**.

These Pedestals need to be placed surrounding the **Imbuement Chamber** or **Enchanting Apparatus**. You can do either a + pattern or an x pattern. Both are valid.

**Use JEI to find the recipe** for each Glyph. Only the Source Gem is consumed, so don't worry about using expensive reagents.

---
# Obtain Some Sourcestone
**Sourcestone** is a material used in magical crafting recipes. It can be created at a basic **Crafting Table**, by surrounding a **Source Gem** with **any kind of Stone**.

---
# Making a Glyph
For starters, let's make the **Harvest Glyph**. As you can see in **JEI**, it requires an **Iron Hoe** and an **Earth Essence**.

To make the Earth Essence, you'll need to have your **Arcane Pedestals set up around your Imbuement Chamber**. You will need **Dirt, Seeds, and an Iron Ingot**. **Right click each reagent on a pedestal** (the order doesn't matter). Once they're in place, put a **Source Gem** into the Imbuement Chamber.

If you have done everything correctly, it will begin crafting! It may go slowly unless there is a **filled Source Jar** within 6 blocks.

Once it's done, pull it from the Chamber and **throw both items** onto your Scribe's Table. Then, grab your finished **Glyph** and **right click while holding it** to unlock the knowledge!

# A more mystical method of crafting
To move forward, you'll want an **Enchanting Apparatus**, which requires an **Arcane Core** beneath it, and **Pedestals** surrounding it.

You can either bury the Arcane Core and place everything on floor-level, or you can place the Core on floor level and raise the Pedestals up.

---
# Craft a Magebloom Seed to get Fiber
**Magebloom** is an intensely magical plant which cannot be found naturally. You must create it using an **Enchanting Apparatus**. It requires **Source Gems** and **Seeds**.

**Place the Source Gems** on 4 of the Pedestals (order doesn't matter), and then **place the Seeds in the Apparatus**.

Magebloom is used in many recipes, but is especially valued for its **Fiber**, from which **mana-infused clothing, and spell parchments** can be made.

**Don't forget to read your Spellbook's documentation in the top left tab!** It has more information than these quests do.

---
# Obtain Novice Robes
Use your newly-acquired **Magebloom Fiber** to craft yourself a set of **Novice Robes**, which will **increase the amount of mana** you have access to.

# New Potion Options
The **Potion Jar** can hold up to 100 doses of a single potion.

You can use it to refill your **Potion Flask**, which is a container able to hold up to 8 doses of the same potion type.

There are two variants of the Potion Flask: **Extend Time** and **Amplify**. I think you can guess what each does.

---
# Potion Melding
The **Potion Melder** allows you to **mix the effects of two or more potions**. It draws from **Potion Jars** which surround it on either side, and when **provided with Source**, outputs to a Jar beneath it.

Only two Potion Jars can be used at a time, however, you can **fill one Jar with a Melded Potion** to add even more effects to it.

**Place it on your Scribe's Table** and then create the desired spell in your Book. You can then **shift right click** on the Table with your Spellbook to write the spell to the wand.

# Special Jars
These two **Jars** each have a unique effect.

The **Jar of Voiding** will delete specified items upon pickup, in exchange for mana. Helpful to get rid of extra Cobblestone! **Shift click** to toggle it on or off. To add an item to the void list, **hold that item in your offhand and right click** with the Jar.

The **Jar of Light** summons a light source to follow the player, which can be dismissed at any time. **Right click** to toggle this summon.

# Ritual of Awakening
The **Weald Walkers** are Archtrees which have been awoken. In order to perform this Ritual, **place the brazier near an Archwood tree** and **right click** it with the Tablet. **Right click** the Brazier to get started.

Once the **Weald Walker** is ambling about, **right click it** with the **Dominion Wand**, and then right click it on the block you want as the Walker's **home**.

# Craft a Ritual Tablet
In order to perform a **Ritual**, you'll need a **Tablet** for the Ritual you want to perform. You can check your **spell book documentation** to find out what each ritual does.

# Summon the Wilden Chimera
In order to make certain items, you'll need to defeat the boss, **The Wilden Chimera**. This does not spawn naturally, and must be summoned via **Ritual**.

It's suggested to be using tier 2 armor and spells before engaging this boss.

To summon him, you'll need to find a **Wilden Hunter**, **Wilden Guardian**, and **Wilden Stalker**, and obtain their **trophies**.

# Obtaining New Familiars
**Familiars** are small creatures who help you with your magical duties. These differ from **Crows** in both how you meet them, and what they can do.

Firstly, you must obtain the **Charm** of each Familiar. This is done differently depending on which one you're trying to summon. But you will need to **activate their Charm** before beginning the Ritual.

You must **place a Ritual Brazier** nearby the Familiar and **put the **Tablet of Binding** on it**. Then **right click** the Brazier. It will ensnare the **Familiar** and produce a **Bound Script**, which you can then **right click** to learn - from then on, that Familiar may be summoned from the **third tab in your spellbook**!

Read on for more information about each Familiar, both in Familiar mode and out.

You can dismiss your summoned Familiar with a **Dispel** Spell. Break the blocks they came from to **get back their Charm**.

---
**Starbuncles** are small, squirrel-like creatures which love **Gold Nuggets**. Find a wild Starbuncle and toss them one to receive a **Starbuncle Shard** in return, which is used to craft its **Charm.** You can simply **right click the charm on the ground** to summon its tame counterpart.

When following you, the **Starbuncle** will grant you **Speed II** and, when fed Gold Nuggets, it will give you the ability to **see gold veins through blocks**.

The **Starbuncle** is capable of **item transport**. Use a **Dominion Wand** to assign it to certain inventories. It will also **harvest fully-grown **Source Berries**.

---
The **Whirlisprig** looks like an unripe tomato helicopter. Once you locate a wild one, **grow a tree near it** in order to receive its **Shards**, used to make its **Charm**! You can then **right click the charm** on any small flower to summon one.

While in Familiar mode, they **reduce the cost of earth spells** by half. Food you eat will also grant you more saturation.

The **Whirlisprig**, when bound to its flower, will **duplicate nearby **organic items** - trees, flowers, etc. They need to be provided with a chest and **Source**.

---
The **Drygmy** is a small gnome-like creature with antlers, and can be found in the wild. In exchange for a **Wilden Horn** dropped at its feet, it will give you a **Drygmy Shard**, which can be used to create its **Charm**. **Right click the Charm on **Mossy Cobblestone**.

When it is following you, it increases your Earth spell damage, and boosts **Looting**.

Multiple **Drygmy** can be assigned to a single **Henge**, and these creatures will **harvest drops and experience gems** from **Livestock** in a 10 block radius, without harming them! After it harvests, it will place items in a nearby inventory, and must recharge its ability **with Source**.

# Take a look, it's in a book
The **Bookwyrm** is a small flying creature that resembles a wyvern, with book pages for wings. You can summon it using a **Bookwyrm Charm** (made with **Iron** and **Source Gems** and **a book**), which you **shift right click on a **Lectern**. After the Lectern finishes drawing magic to itself, the Bookwyrm will appear!

When following you, the **Bookwyrm** **decreases your spell cost and increases your spell damage**.
When it is simply bound to a Lectern, it will **cast spells on the surrounding area**, based on what **Parchment Scrolls** you give it. It can be **dyed** for color-coding purposes. To cast spells, there must be a filled **Source Jar** nearby.

This **item is under construction** and so the quest is temporarily a **checkmark**.

# The world's cutest crafting table
The **Wixie**'s **Shards** are obtained by using the **Dispel** effect on a **Witch** when they're under half health. Once you craft the **Charm**, you can **right click it on a **Cauldron** to summon the **Wixie**.

Wixies will **increase the duration of consumed potions** while they are following you. They will also **apply negative potion effects on enemies**.

When a Wixie is bound to a Cauldron, it will **automatically craft an item or potion** it is told to. It will draw from nearby inventories, and can be paused if it receives a redstone signal.

# I~ can do anything!
There is much more that this mod has to offer! **Runes**, **Enchanter's Weapons**, **Source Relays** and more! Check out the documentation in your **Spell Book** to help you learn even more!

---
The **Amethyst Golem** can be summoned by using the **Ritual of Awakening** on some **Budding Amethyst**.

**Right click** the shard on a block to summon the **Golem**, and then **set its home block** with your **Dominion Wand**.

**Amethyst Golems** will convert Amethyst into **Budding Amethyst**, speed up **amethyst growth**, and will also **harvest amethyst clusters**, so long as their home block is an inventory such as a chest or barrel.

---
**Sirens** can be found rarely in **Ocean Biomes**, and you can obtain their **Shards** by giving them a **Sea Pickle**, their favorite snack!

Use the **Siren Charm** on a piece of Prismarine to summon it. **Sirens** will change color if given **kelp** or **corals**.

The **Siren Shrine** will generate **fishing loot**, as long as it is provided with **Source**. The **Sirens** will produce faster if they have lots of aquatic plants and animals to keep them company!

---
Unlike the other **Charms**, the **Flarecannon**'s does not require **Shards**, and can be simply crafted.

The **Flarecannon** will **defend its territory** much like the **Weald Walkers**, by firing homing projectiles at enemies.

You can give them **Soul Sand** or **Magma Blocks** to change their appearance.

# Fruitful Labors
Each type of **Archwood Tree** bears its own **fruit**! You can find it **growing from the tree like Cocoa Beans**.

The **Bastion Fruit** comes from the **Vexing Archwood** Tree. When consumed, it will grant you **Shielding**, a form of **Resistance**.

The **Frostaya** comes from the **Cascading Archwood** Tree. When consumed, it will **begin to freeze you**.

The **Mendosteen** comes from the **Flourishing Archwood** Tree. It grants the **Recovery** effect, which increases healing you receive from health items.

The **Bombegranate** comes from the **Blazing Archwood** Tree. **Its effects are unknown**, as the journal from which I got this information ends at this entry.

---
The **Vitalic Sourcelink** generates source when it is around **Animals**. When animals **breed, grow up, or die**, it will generate source.

---
The **Mycelial Sourcelink** generates **Source** from **food items**. Provide it with delicious delectables and it will provide you with magic!

It will also **generate mushrooms** and **Mycelium** in the surrounding area.

---
The **Volcanic Sourcelink** produces Source when provided with **Heat**. They will **melt blocks around them** and will also cause **Lava Lilies** to spawn.

Change the color of your **Lava Lily** by placing it over Lava or Magma.

---
The **Alchemical Sourcelink** generates source when provided with **Potions**. The more complex the potion, the more Source is produced.

# Multiple Sets At Once
There are so many options for **magely gear**... How can you choose? Well, you may not have to!

The **Wizard's Armarium** is a **Belt curio** which can contain up to **three sets of armor** and even **hotbar items** for you to swap on the fly.

By default, you'll **press 0** to bring up a radial wheel of armor sets. But **first you need to load the Armarium** with gear.

To begin, **equip your robes and set up your hotbar**. Then, open the radial and **select the slot** to save them to. Open your radial again, and **switch to a different slot**. Equip your second set of armor and then **repeat the previous** steps until all 3 slots are filled. Now, you can **easily swap between them** using the radial.

# Starbuncles Carry Liquid!
Equip your **Starbuncles** with a **Starbucket** to allow them to **transport fluid back and forth**. You may wish to store the fluid in a **Fluid Containment Jar**.

---
The **Starbuncles** are capable of taking lessons from **Bookwyrms** using the **Wyrm Degree**. Now that they're much smarter, they can **operate on only certain sides of their blocks**.

---
The **Starbin** will allow your **Starbuncle** to **delete items it picks up**. Be sure to set a filter first!

You can set a filter by **preparing an Allow/Deny Scroll** and **clicking your Starbuncle** with it.

---
Although it lacks polish, the **Starddle** allows you to do what the best of us have only dreamed of: **riding a giant Starbuncle**.

---
A **Casting Crystal** is an item which can have a **spell bound to it**, and then you can **equip it as a Curio**, and access its spell via a hotkey.

You can **wear up to three** of these, and by default they are accessible with **CTRL+1-3**.

---
Did you know that **Undead** are susceptible to **Spores** and **Grow** spells? You should test it out sometime.

---
**Skyweave** is an item which **mimics the look of the sky**, so you can see sunlight even when underground!
