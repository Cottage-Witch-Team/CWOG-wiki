# Farming Coffee in the Overworld

Coffee beans are found in the Nether and can be planted on magma blocks or basalt.

## Overworld behavior

To produce coffee berries in the Overworld, coffee plants need sacrificial plants around them. Those sacrificial plants eventually become wither roots, so you will need to replace them.

## Automation notes

- Starbuncles and Bookwyrms are not great here due to wither roots.
- Ars Nouveau spell turret attempts did not successfully harvest berries in testing.
- A working setup is two Create deployers (stacked), powered however you prefer.
- Starbuncle wheels can be used for rotational power.
- Use an item collector so deployers do not stall on drops.

## Practical layout

- A full `5x5` sacrificial ring around a centered coffee plant gives longer production before manual reset.
- Minimal rings can work, but reset frequency is higher.

## Tip

`Time in a Bottle` can accelerate coffee growth when needed.
