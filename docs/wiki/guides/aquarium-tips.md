# Aquarium Tips

If you are building an aquarium and want to avoid predation and mob conflicts, use these tested notes.

## Keep separate

- Vanilla fish (`salmon`, `cod`, `pufferfish`, `tropical_fish`) are prey to many mobs.
- Crocodiles and alligator snapping turtles are too aggressive for mixed tanks.
- Frilled sharks attack blobfish and mimic octopus.
- Otters eat koi and vanilla fish.
- Axolotls eat vanilla fish and squids.
- Quark crabs attack other fish.

## Safe mixed tank list

These were tested together without issues:

`platypus`, `seal`, `stradpole`, `axolotl`, `blobfish`, `hammerhead shark`, `lobster`, `mimic octopus`, `warped toad`, `minipad`, `lilytad`, `comb jelly`, `devil's hole pupfish`, `catfish`, `flying fish`, `terrapin`, `ranchu`, `chocolate chip starfish`, `shame faced crab`, `dumbo octopus`, `clam`, `tadpole`, `frog`, `longhorn cowfish`, `harlequin shrimp`, `yeti crab`, `koi`, `sea bunny`, `napoleon wrasse`, `skelewag`, `giant squid`.

## Amphibious mobs

- Warped toads
- Frogs
- Terrapins

## Not aquatic

- Aquaculture turtles
- Rubber duck isopods

## Final warning

Do not put whales in aquariums. They are far larger than expected.
