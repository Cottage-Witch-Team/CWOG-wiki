This page was written for the 1.18 version, but most tips are present in 1.19 as well!

> You can click Advancements in chat to bring them up in the Advancements screen. You can also display your achieved
> Advancements using the Advancement Frame.


> The Akashic Tome has confusing controls. Right click it to select a book from the menu. Left click it in the air to
> reset it from a selected book. Crouch and throw the Tome when a book is selected to remove that book from it. Craft the
> Tome with a book to hold the book inside it.


> You can now harvest Budding Amethyst with Silk Touch!


> You can restore Anvil durability by right clicking it with an iron ingot.


> Blaze Rods are now placeable, just like End Rods!


> If a hay bale is placed underneath a Campfire, it will grant you a height boost when you fly over it with an Elytra.


> Shift+t when hovering over an item in your inventory will share that item in chat for others to check out.


> Compare a piece of gear to what you're wearing by hovering over it and pressing Left Shift!


> Rotten Flesh and Poisonous Potatoes can be placed in the Composter.


> Both Elytra and Totems of Undying can be equipped as Curios (worn accessories, separate from armor). Press + on your
> Numpad to access your Curios inventory.


> Dispensers can now place blocks and seeds!


> In your Controls menu, you can bind your favourite Emotes to a key.


> Rarely, one can find Fairy Rings in Forest or Plains biomes. Dig down through the center of these to find Diamonds or
> Emeralds!


> Item frames can be colored using Dyes!


> Ghasts will sometimes cry tears without being slain, allowing you to collect them without fighting.


> Hoes can harvest crops and grass in a 3x3 or 5x5 area.


> You no longer have to be holding an arrow for an Infinity Bow to work.


> Water buckets can be enchanted with Infinity.


> Hold shift and drag click to deposit multiple things quickly, or hold space and click to transfer an entire inventory
> back and forth.


> Press the middle mouse button in an inventory to sort it. You can also use the buttons on the sides of inventory UIs
> to sort, withdraw, and deposit.


> You can now place items on Lilypads.


> Dropped items will be highlighted with a colored beam of light, to help you find them.


> In multiplayer: Chest and Barrel loot is PER PERSON, meaning that if you leave a looted chest intact, another user can
> get their own stuff from it. Looted chests and barrels will change their textures from gold to silver.


> Obsidian boats can float across lava lakes!


> This modpack supports shaders by default, without using OptiFine! This is thanks to the Oculus mod. I recommend
> Complementary Shaders, by EminGTR.


> You can cycle through paintings by right clicking one on the wall, while holding one in your hand.


> Shift and right click a dog or a bee with an empty hand to pet it.


> Pistons can now push tile entities like Chests, even if they have something in their inventory. They cannot push
> spawners, however.

> You can stunt the growth of an animal by feeding it a Poisonous Potato. Have Forever Kittens! All you needed to do was
> poison a baby.

> You can now place pumpkins, melons, and even chorus fruit inside flower pots.


> New Shulkers can spawn if an Endermite burrows into a Purpur block.


> Shulker boxes preview their contents when hovered over. Right click an item on a Shulker box in your inventory to
> place the item easily inside.


> Right click on a Sign to edit the text.


> You can place candles on skulls to be ULTRA GOTH.


> Different types of slabs can now be mixed! Additionally, you can place slabs vertically on the side of blocks. Hold
> shift to place them normally.


> You can break multiple similar blocks at once by holding the ` key as you mine.


> Villagers will follow you if you are holding an emerald block.


> Using Shears on a Hay Bale will make a Thatch block.


> Try giving your aquatic friends a Slime Ball! For some, such as the Blobfish or Mimic Octopus, it will allow them to
> survive on land!


> Make your flowers bushier by bonemealing them!


> Frequently forget what you were doing? Press N to take notes! You can even pin them, and they'll show up on the side
> of your screen, like a sticky note!


> You can now connect Chains to Fences, as if it were a Lead. Right click the Chain on one fence post, and then right
> click it on a second one to connect them.


> You can reroll enchantments on a specific item by lifting it out of the Enchanting Table and placing it back in.


> Different types of slabs can now be mixed! Additionally, you can place slabs vertically on the side of blocks by
> holding shift.


> Afraid of the void? Don't be! If you drop into the void, you'll be teleported to the top of the sky. Worst case
> scenario, you die on terrestrial impact and can easily recover your grave.


> You can use Time in a Bottle to increase the rate at which Cheese cures.
